# Conversion examples

These are authored demonstrations, not independent model trials. The output
is the requested artifact. Comments below explain the examples for the skill
reader; do not append them to ordinary conversions.

## 1. The complete escalation, not one umbrella word

Source:
> You can do much better than the current answer. Look again, question your
> assumptions, and dig into what is really happening. Do not gloss over unresolved
> difficulties. Look for ways to overcome apparent conflicts without sacrificing
> what matters. Connect ideas from different fields and reorganize the problem to
> reveal possibilities you have not considered. Be bold enough to explore them,
> create a concrete new approach, and seek a breakthrough rather than another
> minor refinement.

Doctrine:
```text
TRANSCEND THAT! USE FRESH EYES! INTERROGATE YOUR ASSUMPTIONS!
BE EXCAVATORY! BE APORETIC!
SEEK SUBLATION WITHOUT SACRIFICING WHAT MATTERS!
BE SYNECTIC! RETOPOLOGIZE THE POSSIBILITY SPACE!
BE AUDACIOUS! OPERATE POIETICALLY! SEEK A SALTATORY ADVANCE!
```

Do not reduce the passage to `BE METANOETIC` unless an umbrella word is requested.
`INTERROGATE YOUR ASSUMPTIONS` retains a distinct source instruction. The source
asks to seek a breakthrough, not to assert that a breakthrough has occurred.

## 2. Reduction with preservation

Source:
> Break the system into its real parts, remove what no longer serves a purpose,
> and put what remains back together without changing externally visible behavior.

Doctrine:
```text
FACTORIZE THE SYSTEM! ABLATE THE RESIDUE! RECOMPOSE THE SURVIVORS!
PRESERVE EXTERNALLY OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOR!
```

Do not replace behavioral preservation with `ISOMORPHIC` unless an invertible
structural correspondence is actually required.

## 3. Conditions, literals, and authority survive

Source:
> Only if `zig build test` passes and I approve, run `$ship`.
> Do not merge the PR. Try the deployment at most 3 times.

Doctrine:
```text
ONLY IF `zig build test` PASSES AND I APPROVE, ACTUATE `$ship`!
DO NOT MERGE THE PR! BOUND DEPLOYMENT ATTEMPTS TO AT MOST 3!
```

This is rewritten text, not permission to run either command. Do not uppercase
`zig build test` or `$ship`, drop either precondition, or add another review gate.

## 4. Bounded ambition, not maximalism

Source:
> Consider an ambitious redesign, but only propose changes; do not edit files.

Doctrine:
```text
CONSIDER AN AUDACIOUS REARCHITECTURE! PROPOSE ONLY; DO NOT EDIT FILES!
```

Do not turn considering a redesign into selecting or implementing it.

## 5. Preserve the relation between exploration and decision

Source:
> First explore radically different approaches. Then choose the one best supported
> by the evidence, including the existing approach if it remains best.

Doctrine:
```text
FIRST DIVERGE AUDACIOUSLY! THEN ADJUDICATE BY EVIDENCE!
RETAIN THE INCUMBENT IF IT REMAINS BEST!
```

Do not turn the exploratory permission into permission to adopt unsupported ideas.

## 6. Non-software ideas

Source:
> Understand the other person's perspective, respect their ability to choose,
> and explain the difficult idea clearly without oversimplifying it.

Doctrine:
```text
BE EMPATHIC! PRESERVE AUTONOMY!
RENDER THE IDEA INTELLIGIBLE WITHOUT OVERSIMPLIFICATION!
```

No architecture, review workflow, or technical metaphor is needed.

## 7. Nouns remain nouns

Source (request: "doctrine words only"):
> source of truth; boundary; operational world; task; factory

Doctrine:
```text
CANON; HOROS; KOSMOS; ERGON; ERGASTERION
```

These use the local concept naming profile. Do not change an already named
repository, API identifier, or public product name on this basis.

## 8. A short qualifier beats false precision

Source:
> Retry the request at most 3 times, and only while the server returns `503`.

Doctrine:
```text
RETRY AT MOST 3 TIMES, ONLY WHILE THE SERVER RETURNS `503`!
```

The compact command is already an adequate doctrine phrase. `BE RESILIENT` or
`BE IDEMPOTENT` would change the meaning, even though they sound more specialized.

## 9. Investigation is not a claim of cause

Source:
> Observe when the agent succeeds and fails; investigate whether the prompt
> causes the difference rather than assuming it does.

Doctrine:
```text
OBSERVE SUCCESS AND FAILURE ETHOLOGICALLY!
INVESTIGATE THE PROMPT'S CAUSAL ROLE; DO NOT PRESUME IT!
```

## 10. Decode, do not reverse polarity

Source (request: "invert this back into plain language"):
> BE APORETIC! OPERATE POIETICALLY!

Plain language:
> Do not rush past genuine unresolved difficulties. Create a new form or approach.

## 11. Requested professional register

Source (request: "doctrine style, no shouting"):
> Adapt your method when evidence changes, but keep the objective fixed.

Doctrine:
> Be protean in method when the evidence changes; remain steadfast in purpose.

## 12. A genuine conflict must stay visible

Source:
> Preserve every line exactly and reduce the file's line count by half.

Doctrine:
```text
PRESERVE EVERY LINE EXACTLY. HALVE THE FILE'S LINE COUNT.
```

Necessary note:
> For a nonempty file, these requirements conflict under the ordinary meaning of
> preserving every line. The rewrite retains the conflict rather than resolving it.

## 13. Composition beyond the seed bank

Source:
> Keep the central image alive, vary its presentation, and bring it back at the end.

Doctrine:
```text
SUSTAIN THE MOTIF! VARY ITS EXPRESSION! REPRISE IT AT THE CLOSE!
```

The vocabulary comes from the task's artistic meaning, not a coding-agent stack.

## 14. Preserve uncertainty and narrow scope

Source:
> This change may improve latency for small inputs, but we have not measured it.

Doctrine:
```text
POSSIBLE LATENCY GAIN FOR SMALL INPUTS; EMPIRICAL STATUS: UNMEASURED.
```

An observation is not silently turned into a command, a universal claim, or proof.
