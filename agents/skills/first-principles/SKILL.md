Start over from first principles. Set aside the current framing, solution, abstractions, conventions, analogies, and inherited constraints. State the irreducible outcome and the evidence that would establish it; separate established facts and necessary constraints from assumptions, hypotheses, preferences, and contingent choices; identify the governing invariants and causal mechanisms. Make the surviving premises explicit as a minimal axiom basis: label each as an observed fact, necessary constraint, chosen objective, or irreducible postulate; test each for necessity and independence and the set for consistency and sufficiency; distinguish axioms from definitions and derived claims; and admit no conclusion without a traceable derivation. Then derive the smallest sufficient approach upward from only that basis. Compare the derivation with any current approach and expose every hidden or unsupported premise, accidental constraint, unnecessary component, contradiction, and missed possibility. Be AXIOMATIC—RUTHLESSLY foundational, derivational, and exacting!

## Application

Suspend the incumbent's claimed necessity, not established evidence or accepted
obligations. Keep user-owned outcomes, source-fixed decisions, compatibility,
authority, resource limits, and independently required procedures binding. A
required outcome is an obligation to establish, not an observed fact. Keep
unresolved assumptions and postulates conditional, with material scope explicit;
lack of a known alternative does not establish necessity.

Use analogies and existing knowledge to generate hypotheses, not certify them.
Derivation establishes consequences relative to premises; it does not by itself
establish empirical validity or a uniquely necessary design. Same-context
reconsideration needs no separate worker and makes no rationale-isolation claim.

Test sufficiency against the complete source-required relationship and
required-valid domain. Derive obligations from authoritative facts and permitted
operations, not only the current example or candidate-supplied collection. A
coherent argument for a weaker property does not establish the required one.
Minimize unsupported commitments, not the number of listed premises or
independent sources of evidence.

Compare adequate constructions under the receiving task's criteria; do not claim
global minimality without support. Preserve an adequate incumbent or material
underdetermination rather than manufacture a change or uniquely necessary answer.
Revise model-owned premises when evidence changes them without silently weakening
accepted requirements. Reuse applicable derivations rather than add a separate
pass to settled work; explicit invocation still applies the canonical passage.

Stop within task limits when the material question is sufficiently answered, or
state the remaining uncertainty and a useful deciding observation when available.
Return the useful consequence and its supporting premises through the existing
workflow, not a reasoning transcript or mandatory axiom report. This pass grants
no new effects or review credit: selection, experiments, mutation, and closure
remain with the receiving workflow or the user's task authorization.

When interactions, accumulated state, or delayed effects determine whether the
basis is sufficient, use [systemic-premises.md](references/systemic-premises.md)
within this pass. These causal distinctions support the unchanged canonical
passage; they add no separate procedure.
