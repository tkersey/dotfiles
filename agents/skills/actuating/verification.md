# Verification construction

Use when designing or reducing materially costly verification, diagnosing slow
qualification, or choosing a broad retry while verification cost impedes the Goal.
Keep [proof acceptance](SKILL.md#proof-acceptance) authoritative. Reuse the Working
Set, proof inventory, native build graph, and existing logs; no new registry,
review lane, or mandatory measurement artifact is needed. Apply relevant
[Tune performance guidance](../tune/performance.md) within Actuating's existing
selection, effect, and completion authority, without starting a second workflow.

## Preserve the deciding evidence

For the expensive work being changed, identify its source-backed obligation and
distinct contribution: the failure it can expose and the independence, scale,
sequence, lifetime, or environment needed to expose it. Derive the necessary
distinctions from the source and reachable mechanisms, not only known defects or
the candidate's surviving test list. Do not make every incidental old assertion
an obligation or require a suite-wide equivalence proof for a local reduction.

Match the argument to the change:

| Change | Deciding preservation argument |
|---|---|
| Remove an identical invocation | Equivalent inputs, fresh starting conditions, and observations; repetition has no required statistical, scheduling, or lifecycle contribution. Accept direct duplication promptly. |
| Shrink a composed fixture | The affected path delegates the scale-sensitive behavior to the tested owner without adding an untested transformation. A test elsewhere is insufficient without that relationship. |
| Consolidate failure or resource cases | Preserve the relevant disturbance, branch cardinality, sequence, peak live state, and bound. Branch inclusion, equal counts, or a larger mixed input alone do not establish resource dominance. |

Inspect and execute only what resolves the affected distinction. If preservation
cannot be established cheaply, retain the discriminating witness and reduce its
surrounding work. Minimize setup while retaining assertions that the required
state or transition was reached; early completion that bypasses it earns no credit.

## Construct the necessary work

Share immutable preparation while constructing fresh mutable sessions. Preserve
independent expectations, consumer execution, and independently exercised build
paths where their independence is part of the claim. Shared production logic
cannot independently certify itself.

Locate exhaustive failure injection at the transaction owning the guarantee.
Retain integrated evidence where a caller adds partial commit, custody, lifetime,
capability consumption, or retry obligations. Establish the delegation and caller
interaction before removing repeated lower-level sweeps; owner coverage alone
does not prove composition or preparation excluded from the new sweep.

Bind artifact reuse to its actual build inputs, including toolchain, target,
flags, generated inputs, and embedded assets. Preserve provenance and consumer
identity through existing mechanisms. Reusable compilation is distinct from fresh
verification: changed checks must execute, and a cache hit is not a passing result.
Narrow input identity from the dependency closure, not file-extension guesses.

Make consumers depend on the artifact's actual ready-and-authenticated event;
keep independent checks required at final acceptance without unnecessarily
blocking consumer startup. Respect the provider's scheduling semantics. Compare
sharing with its transfer, setup, and serialization costs; fewer builds or jobs
alone do not establish a shorter workflow or lower total cost.

## Choose the next execution

After unsuccessful or inconclusive terminal qualification, apply the
[progress assessment](recurrence.md#progress-before-another-iteration) before
another correction or broad retry. Carry comparable attempts across candidate
heads; distinguish a live check's observation timeout from a terminal result.

Use existing phase observations and actual dependencies to assess all currently
plausible limiting paths. Separate setup, compilation, execution, transfer,
queueing, and finalization; distinguish workflow elapsed time from total runner
work. Before selecting a change, estimate which paths it shortens and which
unchanged path could become limiting. Include preservation, measurement, and
infrastructure maintenance effort when judging the expected benefit.

Resolve an isolated uncertainty with the smallest useful execution: an affected
fixture, owner transaction, cold build, or producer-consumer path. Reuse applicable
diagnostic evidence instead of obtaining another whole-workflow baseline for each
edit. Avoid a broad retry while existing evidence identifies an unaffected
obstruction. A broad diagnostic remains appropriate when a specific whole-workflow
effect cannot be resolved more cheaply; identify what its result will change.

This selects the next diagnostic execution; it does not reduce accepted required
verification. Retain every required check for final qualification under
[proof acceptance](SKILL.md#proof-acceptance). Resume or rerun an unfinished
required check once its obstruction is addressed; if blocked, report the blocker
and incomplete status. Focused results cannot substitute for the required run.

Use complete qualification when the cumulative candidate plausibly fits the
accepted job and workflow limits, accounting for known overhead and observed
variation. Keep required cold and cache-assisted modes distinct. Complete required
verification, retention, and terminal checks on the accepted candidate; a finished
fixture inside a cancelled workflow remains diagnostic evidence. Structure and
phase estimates do not prove wall-clock compliance. Stop optional experiments
when the Goal is established or further evidence cannot change the decision;
report remaining obstructions without raising ceilings or weakening obligations.
