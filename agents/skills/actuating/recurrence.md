# Recurrence and progress

## Recurrence and progress

Separate claim revocation from construction reconsideration. An exact current
entailed `same-claim` witness revokes the exclusion claim under
[post-elimination-falsification.md](references/post-elimination-falsification.md).
Reopen the causal explanation and replay its applicable history before selecting
the successor. Source-domain omissions and authority failures cannot be dismissed
as missing assertions. Same broad law or owner alone does not establish recurrence.
A new head, route label, or family name does not erase contrary evidence.

Reconsider the boundary when source evidence shows another supported operation
retains the enabling freedom under an unresolved Goal obligation, even if the
earlier narrow claim remains true. Inconsistent interpretations across sanctioned
phases can expose that relationship without refuting the earlier family claim:
which admission-relevant distinction was lost or independently reconstructed?
Preserve Review Fold's exact classification; do not invent same-claim recurrence or
new requirements. A defensible narrow claim does not discharge the wider accepted
Goal. Existing effect authority and review freezes govern any successor work.

Judge progress by the supported failure mechanism excluded, required-valid behavior
preserved, sanctioned paths covered, and independently maintained truths retired.
Distinguish a supported composition that follows from existing primitive rules
from one that needs another independent semantic interpretation or special case.
Learning that an explanation or oracle was wrong is useful without itself proving
the code better. More findings can be valuable discovery; fewer findings, shorter
runs, smaller diffs, and clean streaks do not by themselves establish efficacy.
Use the existing [offline comparison](references/counterexample-guided-normalization.md#behavioral-falsifier)
to assess additions and ablations, not a new review lane, runtime score, or store.

## Progress before another iteration

For transient review-execution failures, use
[review recovery](references/review-contract.md#review-recovery). A supported
transient hypothesis can justify an unchanged request after backoff; ordinary
recovery does not require architectural reconsideration or a new code finding.
Reassess the approach when the accumulated evidence no longer supports it.

After a required verifier terminates unsuccessfully or inconclusively, and after
an invalidated review epoch closes, compare the recent attempts with the last
supported progress before another correction, broad retry, or successor review
wave. On resumption, recover this history before repeating failed work. An
admitted code liability or proved same-family recurrence is not required to
question the investigation or approach; preserve Review Fold's classifications.

Keep a compact history in the existing Working Set: closed review epochs (including
superseded batches with incomplete inquiry) and
relevant terminal verification attempts, the unresolved obligation or obstruction,
each relevant intervention and its predicted versus observed result, the last
supported progress, and the evidence basis of the latest shared activation. For
each successor, add its production lines added and deleted and its change in
[deciding sites](construction.md#initial-construction) for each law it touched.
Reference existing owner results rather than copying logs or adding a store.
Preserve this history across new heads, clean-credit resets, changed finding labels,
delegation, and compaction. Reclassify applicability when the accepted source
changes; preserve provenance and mark unavailable history unknown, never zero.

Count a closed review epoch once, separately from its lenses, findings, and clean
confirmations; retain which epochs were invalidated. Count comparable terminal
verification executions, not polls or observation timeouts on a live check.
Group one matrix run's reports of the same obstruction as one attempt without
discarding distinct evidence. A newly reached check or an experiment that resolves
a material uncertainty can be progress while qualification remains incomplete.

Treat a failed remedy prediction, materially the same obstruction after its
attempted correction, cycling between repairs, growing compensators around an
unresolved obligation, or successive attempts without relevant improvement or
deciding evidence as a new Metanoetic trigger. Apply the
[shared Glaze then Metanoetic activation](architecture.md) afresh before repeating
the approach. Do not require a new defect identity or causal family to recognize
that the earlier framing no longer justifies the next attempt.

Use two relevant unsuccessful terminal verification attempts without deciding new
evidence, and each third completed invalidated review epoch, as backstops for an
explicit assessment of the accumulated attempts, even when symptoms differ or
each local edit seems reasonable. These are attention thresholds, not proof of
stagnation, automatic passage reinjection, a redesign requirement, or round limits.
Act on an earlier causal trigger without waiting for either count. Applying a
skill, making a commit, or passing an unrelated check is not supported progress.

Escalate when an admitted defect lies in a deciding site that an intervention in
this history added, or comes from the same supported causal mechanism as a defect
an earlier intervention addressed: that construction did not hold. Add no deciding
site for it; fix the sole site, delegate to the law's owner, or retire or unify the
implicated sites under the [deciding-site rule](construction.md#initial-construction).
Select that construction with
[independent reconstruction](references/independent-reconstruction.md) within its
authority limits.

Reconsider the implicated mechanism, assumptions, oracle, environment, verification
arrangement, or task decomposition while keeping the accepted Goal fixed. Select
a next step that changes a relevant intervention or resolves a stated uncertainty.
Use [premise-guided investigation](../universalist/references/claim-appropriate-proof.md#premise-guided-investigation)
when an unsettled premise or omitted source-permitted behavior could change that choice.
Retain the incumbent when evidence justifies continuing it. Reuse an earlier
activation only while its basis still addresses the current evidence. Do not
repeat an equivalent attempt with neither a relevant change nor a deciding
observation. A known external blocker leaves dependent work incomplete; continue
independent authorized work rather than manufacturing a code defect or retrying
unchanged conditions.

Keep this assessment within existing selection, not a new stage or report. During
an open review epoch, record evidence and reconsider read-only; close it under the
[applicable epoch rule](review-closeout.md#review-epoch-immutability-and-evidence-acquisition)
before successor mutation. Supersession is not completed inquiry or proof of progress. Preserve required behavior, resource
ceilings, final verification, and review inventory. Neither an attention threshold
nor a fresh activation grants proof, review credit, or completion.
