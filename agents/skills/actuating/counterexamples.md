# Observe and adjudicate

## Observe and adjudicate

Apply the root's [proof acceptance](SKILL.md#proof-acceptance), including exact
subject binding and verifier-integrity checks. They have one authority here, not a
second counterexample-specific formulation.

Before counterexample-driven selection, reconcile relevant executor, self-review,
provider/PR, test, incident, migration, compatibility, verifier, and corpus evidence.
Each live source is folded, proven non-current, or explicitly unavailable. An omitted
live finding keeps the cut open; an unavailable source makes the affected horizon
incomplete, not clean. Missing evidence blocks only claims or actions that depend
on it; do not make provider availability a prerequisite for initial implementation.

Have `$review-fold` project the corpus, preserve original subjects, re-evaluate
current applicability, and classify proposed laws:

```text
entailed          current witness may create correctness pressure
strengthening     non-blocking follow-up unless accepted authority adopts it
preference        reject as a current correctness liability
new-requirement   reopen Goal authority
underdetermined   seek authority; no correctness mutation from the claim
```

Have Review Fold apply its [counterexample admission](../review-fold/SKILL.md#counterexample-admission)
for review findings and failed checks before treating either as a liability.
Self-discovered review findings use the same admission; preserve their provenance
without inventing a CAS receipt or waiving admission because Actuating found them.
A failed execution stays failed; its diagnosis is adjudicated, not its history.
A discrepancy may expose a defect in the implementation, the witness's interpretation, an oracle, or a proof/assessment
claim. Establish which obligation actually fails; do not default to changing
production code or to trusting the checker. A refuted allegation returns to Review
Fold; an oracle correction needs its own source-grounded obligation and independent
evidence, never changed expectations merely to agree with the candidate. Unresolved
premises justify authorized investigation, not an invented repair. Acceptance of
a witness proves neither the proposed fix nor its claimed causal family.

Only current accepted witnesses authorize counterexample response. Duplicate
observations add provenance, not independent failures. Historical absence cannot
prove first occurrence, disjointness, or elimination under an incomplete horizon.
Review Fold captures newly accepted independent witnesses only with enclosing
corpus-write authority. Pass `corpus_write_authorized` on every handoff: true when
that effect is authorized; `corpus_write_authorized: false` for `analyze` or absent
authority. Read-only analysis may project and adjudicate available evidence but
never changes the subject, captures new records, or provisions tools for capture.
Ledger's automatic custody preparation precedes projection independently of that
capture flag, under its own explicit file/custody-maintenance constraints.
Other routes inherit their actual effect scope; supporting skills cannot expand it.
Actuating neither copies the corpus nor persists its current interpretation.

A rejected strengthening must not enter code to placate a reviewer or protect a
clean streak. Reopen authority or remove it. Correcting an unsupported public
overclaim may follow an existing truthfulness obligation; weakening an accepted
requirement, compatibility promise, or release posture needs explicit authority.

## Compile the first loss of guarantee

Use [initial construction](SKILL.md#initial-construction) for the repair; there is
no separate construction pipeline. On returning to construction, form the
[next assignment](SKILL.md#construction-working-set) from the accepted source and
the full admitted basis. Combine current and applicable historical witnesses under
the source-required law. Carry every admitted liability into the
root's [automatic construction reconsideration](SKILL.md#mission). Reuse inquiry
across witnesses only when the same supported causal mechanism covers them; account
for each new witness and reopen the explanation when it adds a causal distinction.
A shared reviewer, file, owner, or law alone does not establish a common cause.
Reconsideration belongs to response selection, not a separate pass or a demand to
redesign. Acceptance establishes a supported
disagreement, not the causal explanation. Investigate the implementation, model,
oracle, and claim as evidence warrants; a completed family narrative or nomination
is not an entry condition for inquiry.

Before selecting a counterexample-driven change, remove the report's confidence,
repetition, and preferred patch from its justification while retaining independently
established evidence. Identify the current accepted obligation that still supports
the contemplated semantic change. A rejected or materially unresolved allegation
cannot supply that basis merely because its proposed correction seems safer or
would satisfy a reviewer. A separately supported, authorized obligation may justify
the same edit; initial implementation still derives directly from the accepted Goal.

Carry the disposition, decisive evidence, unresolved premises, and their effect on
permitted corrective work in the existing assignment through delegation, resumption,
and compaction. Do not reduce a rejected allegation to a task title saying to fix it.
During the existing diff/proof inspection, compare actual changes to that basis,
including test expectations, check selection, compatibility, and completion claims.
Correct rejection in prose does not excuse an unsupported change. This adds no
packet, review lane, or separate pass; reuse an adequate source argument or observation.

Establish the affected domain from the source, not only the reported path or supplied
records. Before realizing a selected candidate, choose a source-supported
mechanism discriminator under [proof acceptance](SKILL.md#proof-acceptance).
Before crediting a new or materially changed regression as deciding evidence,
establish that it detects the particular source-backed violation claimed repaired.
Reuse an applicable failing execution or decisive independent source argument.
Otherwise use the smallest safe negative control: run the focused witness on the
faulty predecessor, or on an isolated copy with only the implicated defect restored.
Keep the source-derived expectation fixed. The control must reach the claimed
transition and expose its relevant wrong observation; an earlier rejection,
unrelated build failure, skipped check, or timeout does not establish sensitivity.
Use a targeted control when whole-predecessor failure cannot isolate that claim.

If the witness still passes, correct its setup, selection, or assertion from the
accepted source before crediting it. Do not change production semantics merely to
kill an artificial mutant. The control must represent an admitted violation, not
require a particular implementation. Confirm the same witness passes on the actual
repaired candidate. Reaching a phase alone does not prove sensitivity to its defect;
passing this control does not prove general coverage or required-valid preservation.

Keep these executions in the existing proof inventory. Reuse adequate evidence;
there is no per-test or per-entry-point quota, new review lane, or fixed reasoning
sequence. Controls require mutation authority, safe isolation, and no open review
epoch; never contaminate the delivered candidate. If a safe, proportionate control
is unavailable, use a decisive independent source argument or leave the affected
coverage claim unresolved. This neither waives required verification nor narrows
the accepted Goal.

When a controlled comparison could settle a material disputed premise, keep the
source-backed obligation and original allegation fixed. Distinguish an observed
behavior change from a changed reviewer judgment. Establish that a purported repair
reaches the relevant behavior and that a defect-preserving control really preserves
it; a build failure, unavailable premise, or earlier rejection is inconclusive for
that comparison. A failed proposed repair does not refute a supported finding.
Choose diagnostic changes to isolate the uncertainty, not to prescribe the production
patch. Claim withdrawal proves neither required-valid preservation nor family-wide
elimination. Reuse decisive evidence and the existing authority/freeze rules above;
comparisons are optional techniques, not an intervention quota or acceptance gate.

When preservation through subsequent behavior is uncertain, inspect source-permitted
operations that can overwrite, invalidate, or reinterpret a premise of the proposed
guarantee. Use a relevant operation or composition to discriminate that uncertainty;
follow semantic dependencies, not shared fields alone. Reuse adequate native
preservation evidence when it already settles the question; do not invent another
writer, sibling, or test obligation.

Distinguish model adequacy, required semantic content, and operation coverage.
When the model erases a distinction required by the law, retain or derive the
missing distinction. Derive required records from retained obligations, not merely
from supplied members. A checked constructor, shared predicate, exhaustive switch,
or new type alone establishes neither meaning, required presence, nor coverage.

Prefer making an equivalent omission unavailable to other operations over adding
another branch-specific check. Preserve required-valid behavior without merging
away independent verification. A local correction can establish a reusable native
scope or transition rule when the existing construction owns the law. Independent
history, authority, effects, and progress must not disappear merely to unify code.

The [construction repertoire](references/counterexample-guided-normalization.md)
and [feedback analysis](references/feedback-failures.md) offer techniques for
uncertain models, interacting phases, or aggregate behavior. Their reasoning moves
are selectable; claim-specific evidence and the root's common proof obligations
remain binding. Stop inquiry when further source-supported investigation cannot
change the selected mechanism or its required proof. Samples discriminate an
explanation; they do not prove an open-domain exclusion.
