# Live Closure

Closure is a current judgment over owner facts, not stored workflow state:

```text
Close(Goal, Git, validation, review execution evidence, ReviewFoldCorpus, Ship, current evidence)
-> continue | ready-to-ship | complete | blocked
```

`implement` requires local realization and proof, not review or publication. Bare
Actuating and `review-closeout` additionally require publication and convergence.
`analyze` never mutates or claims closure. No review epoch may remain open at
closure; an epoch freezes its exact candidate, not initial implementation.

## Authority and source horizon

Use [counterexample admission](../counterexamples.md#observe-and-adjudicate) for
law authority, live-source reconciliation, corpus applicability, and corrective
effects. Unavailable evidence limits dependent claims; it is not a blanket provider
or history prerequisite for initial implementation. Correcting an unsupported
claim cannot waive accepted behavior.

## Construction adequacy

Apply the [common proof obligations](../SKILL.md#realization-and-common-proof-obligations)
and [required verification](../construction.md#proof-acceptance) to the exact head.
Closure consumes their evidence, not a second construction procedure. Reuse valid
proof; invalidated subjects, assumptions, domains, or claims require the affected
proof again. Use the construction repertoire only when its argument is needed.

## Reviewability and completion

A candidate remains `realizing` until the selected construction, authority-complete
diff, migrations, retirements, and all required proof are complete. Only then may
it become `reviewable`. A newly discovered required proof after convergence
invalidates the prior credit; the missing required checks must finish successfully
before fresh review. A disclosed limitation does not make an unfinished required
check complete.

For full `complete`, require:

```text
every required check finished successfully against the delivered state
required Ship/provider state matches the exact head
no review epoch remains open; all terminal semantic outcomes are folded
all five changeset auxiliary obligations have terminal, adjudicated, credited outcomes
all six lens obligations for each selected file have terminal, adjudicated, credited outcomes
five consecutive distinct changeset standard cleans credited on the unchanged head under the selected execution mode and bound policy
no unresolved current accepted liability
no unauthorized semantic change, containment, narrowing, risk, or deferral
all completeness and historical claims match their actual evidence horizon
no later falsifier of the Goal, family, mechanism, proof, publication, or review
```

[Review and closure](../review-closeout.md#review-and-closure) owns convergence,
resets, and scope obligations; [Review-credit admission](review-contract.md#review-credit-admission)
owns both accounting and user/PR narration. A held required obligation remains
incomplete even when receipts say `clean`. Keep material limitations and the
authorized bounded or non-complete outcome when required evidence is unavailable.
Executor or standard-policy changes require a newly bound campaign; never mix
native and subagent streaks.

An exact same-claim witness revokes family exclusion immediately. A sanctioned
path absent from the source-derived coverage basis revokes that claim. Reopen the causal
explanation; neither a local label nor equal opaque digests can preserve the claim.

A locally complete implementation awaiting required publication is `ready-to-ship`.
An accepted unresolved or deferred liability is not unqualified `complete`.
Continue or rerun outstanding required verification while execution is possible.
Return `blocked` with incomplete status for missing authority, unresolved
causal/domain evidence needed for the requested claim, incomplete cuts, missing required proof, unowned residuals,
stale reviews, or publication mismatch. Block the affected claim, not unrelated
already-authorized implementation.
