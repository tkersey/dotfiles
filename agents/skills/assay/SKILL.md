# Assay

Determine what the evidence establishes about the accepted purpose, for the
subject actually assessed. Return a supported conclusion, a concrete
contradiction, or the exact unresolved obligation. Keep the conclusion no
stronger than its evidence.

## Authority and scope

Inherit the task's requirements, authority, evidence standard, budget, and
stopping condition. Assessment alone authorizes no source repair, experiment,
installation, publication, changed acceptance rule, or additional review gate.
Use existing authorized observations and runners; continue independent assessment
when one necessary observation is unavailable.

Own qualification of the named claim. Cybernating owns investigation of textual
source contributions and authorized optimization; implementation, review,
adoption, and publication retain their existing owners. An enclosing workflow
can consume this assessment without starting a separate campaign.

Work directly with native evidence. The Assay CLI is optional: when an installed
CLI could reduce repeated assessment work, read [CLI use](references/cli.md).
Do not require its implementation or installation to answer an otherwise
decidable question.

## Assess the claim

1. **Bind the question.** Recover the accepted obligation and the relevant
   subject, revision, conditions, and scope from current evidence. Distinguish
   an artifact satisfying checks, a repair preserving required behavior, an
   evaluator measuring its target, and a procedure producing better outcomes.
   A requested causal explanation is a separate claim. Resolve discoverable
   facts before asking the user; do not invent a criterion or loss allowance.
2. **Inspect what happened.** Read the actual artifact, authoritative state,
   execution result, or retained witness needed for the claim. Keep observations
   separate from an agent's account and a grader's interpretation. Bind effective
   loaded instructions when their behavior is the subject; authored text alone
   does not establish delivery. Missing identity or provenance limits only the
   conclusions that depend on it.
3. **Check applicability and discrimination.** Verify that evidence concerns
   this subject, obligation, and relevant conditions. Ask whether it preserves
   the distinction the decision requires. If assessment adequacy is disputed,
   or the evaluator is the subject, read
   [evaluator assessment](references/evaluator.md). Do not launch that deeper
   investigation for every passing check.
4. **Assess obligations before aggregation.** Keep required behavior and valid
   alternatives visible. Apply the accepted rule, including any declared
   thresholds; do not invent a perfect-score requirement. An improved average
   cannot discharge a separately violated obligation. For comparative or
   procedure claims, read [comparisons](references/comparisons.md).
5. **Return the bounded result.** State what is supported, refuted, or unresolved,
   with decisive evidence and any remaining required observation. Separate the
   target's fitness from completion of this assessment: a read-only inquiry can
   finish with a refutation or an honest unresolved result.

## Preserve the deciding distinctions

Execution state and judgment are separate. A completed evaluation can refute a
claim or leave it unresolved. An interrupted run can supply a decisive
counterexample, while supplying no evidence for its unfinished checks. A timeout
can refute a declared deadline; it does not automatically refute unrelated
correctness. Never count missing, stopped, or ungraded work as a clean result.
Retain attempts, exclusions, and denominators when they affect the conclusion.

Derive expected behavior from the accepted task and independent domain evidence.
A candidate cannot silently redefine its own assessment. Keep an authorized
contract amendment distinct from success under the previous contract. Challenge
an allegation and its proposed repair separately; rejecting required inputs or
blocking all progress can violate the purpose.

Accept any construction preserving the required observations unless the task
also constrains its representation or path. Structural ownership, compatibility,
scope, or resource claims can be assessed from the relevant artifact and accepted
rule; do not manufacture a runtime failure. Construction guarantees retain their
premises and their connection to execution. Finite tests do not become universal
proofs, and a model judgment does not become an independently observed effect.

Reuse evidence only within its established applicability. Start from the actual
assessed subject; carry evidence to a successor only with a justified preservation
basis. Regrading retained observations changes a judgment, not the historical
execution. If the new question needs an unrecorded distinction, obtain an
authorized observation or leave that claim unresolved.

When applicable reports conflict, reconcile their subject, contract, conditions,
and observation reliability. Preserve contradictions that remain; do not choose
a favorable verdict. A valid counterexample can refute its scoped claim despite
other successes. Conflicting observations of the same event leave the affected
claim unresolved until their reliability is settled.

## Finish

Stop when the named claim is decided to the requested strength and required
checks are satisfied, or identify the precise evidence, authority, or budget
gap. Do not prolong bounded work to prove a general procedure advantage that
was never required. Useful local repair evidence need not isolate every causal
contribution.

Return a concise assessment in the task's existing output or record: the claim
and subject; supported, refuted, and unresolved obligations; decisive evidence;
and the next required observation only when one remains. Omit empty fields.
Preserve private evidence in its existing store; retain durable lessons through
the selected learning or custody owner when applicable, without creating another
mandatory record.

For package validation, use [workflow probes](references/workflow-probes.md);
they are authored acceptance cases, not a runtime checklist or measured efficacy.
