# Compare candidates and procedures

Use for a claim of improvement, preservation across successors, or a procedure's
effectiveness. Retain the accepted objective and tradeoffs.

## Match the unit to the claim

An artifact assessment concerns the identified artifact. A repair assessment
compares the demonstrated defect and required-valid behavior across successors.
A procedure assessment concerns complete episodes that produce and qualify
artifacts. Repeated checks on one artifact are not independent workflow episodes.

Compare relevant cases, initial state, tools, effective instructions, model
settings, evaluators, budgets, and stopping rules. Different conditions may be
the intended intervention: identify it, and limit attribution accordingly.
Missing settings need not invalidate a directly observed artifact failure;
they can leave a comparative or population claim unresolved.

Preserve every attempt, exclusion, timeout, and failed observation with the
relevant denominator. Count retries, unsuccessful candidates, coordination,
and verification when the question concerns total episode cost. Unknown cost
is unknown, not zero. Faster failure does not establish faster successful work.

## Preserve obligations before selecting

Compare per-obligation transitions as well as any accepted aggregate. Check
the delivered candidate, not only its best earlier checkpoint. Different
failure families can have the same score. A local improvement can coexist
with a violated preservation obligation.

Keep mandatory requirements separate from preferences. Use the supplied
comparison policy and any accepted loss margin; do not create a weighted score,
invent an accuracy tradeoff, or interpret a statistically inconclusive
difference as equivalence. Preserve genuinely incomparable alternatives.

A candidate can satisfy bounded acceptance while its detailed causal mechanism
remains unresolved. Do not demand causal decomposition, population reliability,
or indefinite longitudinal observation unless that is part of the claim.

## Preserve experimental meaning

Record whether observations are development or confirmation evidence and how
candidate selection used them. Feedback used to revise the candidate or grader
becomes development evidence for its successor; a new identifier does not reset
exposure. Reuse the accepted sampling and stopping policy. Missing policy limits
the stronger claim rather than authorizing retrospective thresholds.

Identify dependence from shared sessions, cases, caches, infrastructure, or
selection. Repetitions and multiple reviewers do not automatically supply
independent confirmations. Use uncertainty and comparison methods proportionate
to the accepted claim; no universal sample count is implied.

Report what improved, regressed, or remains unresolved and why the subjects are
comparable. A valid comparison operation does not itself authorize adoption or
prove that one candidate is preferable.
