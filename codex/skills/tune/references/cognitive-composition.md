# Cognitive Composition for Skill Authoring

Read when a proposed cognitive instruction needs more than one distinct
operation. The purpose is to author a coherent native procedure, not to install
an operator runner, mandatory stack, or new workflow around the target.

## Composition admission

Prefer an existing native rule, no change, or one sufficient operation. Combine
operations only when each has a distinct necessary role, their ordering matters,
they serve the same bounded objective and evidence, and one operation cannot
produce the expected delta. Synonym count is not composition.

Reuse the target's existing selector, construction method, proof boundary, and
stop whenever sufficient. A new search operation does not require recreating
those downstream stages. More doctrine does not establish more intelligence.

## Ordering laws

Apply only the dependencies live in the proposed change, not every row as a
mandatory pipeline. A role already discharged by current evidence need not run
again.

| Dependency | Reason and boundary |
|---|---|
| Context binding before evidence-sensitive inference | Situate or rebaseline the exact subject; do not excavate, adjudicate, or prove against stale state |
| Defamiliarization before independent derivation | Suspend inherited framing, then derive from retained facts; reset must not discard verified evidence |
| Excavation before structural selection when ownership is unclear | Find the enabling owner, representation, or invariant before choosing the intervention; skip descent when that layer is established |
| Aporia before adjudication, not instead of it | Name the contradiction and decisive distinction, then resolve, justify a reversible decision, or report obstruction |
| Retopology before generation when adjacency is the bottleneck | Change the candidate neighborhood under a structural warrant before searching it; relevance grants no authority |
| Candidate generation before selection | New or ambitious forms still face the same criteria; novelty cannot select itself |
| Accepted premises and architecture before derivational realization | Select governing organization, encode invariants where possible, then derive the witness; do not reopen source-fixed decisions gratuitously |
| Obligation analysis and ablation before normalization | Remove or identify unearned factors and recompose survivors; the preservation relation constrains rather than performs the reduction |
| Selection and authorization before actuation | Do not execute an unselected or unauthorized candidate; once selected and legal, stop generating merely to defer action |
| State movement before a closure claim | Current proof and a reopen condition still govern closure; movement alone is insufficient |

A governor may constrain several operations throughout the procedure. It is not
necessarily a separate sequential pass. Likewise, proof relations and desired
outcomes belong on the operations they constrain, not in a list of actions.

## Composition examples

These show dependencies to translate into the target, not canonical invocation
sequences or an instruction to add every named role.

### First-principles reconstruction

Suspend an unsupported inherited frame, separate observations from assumptions,
then derive from the smallest defensible premise set. Reuse premises already
established by authoritative evidence; familiarity alone does not invalidate them.

### Search for a different mechanism

Bind the actual objective, change which mechanisms are reachable under a
structural warrant, then compare organizations using the target's existing
selector. A discontinuous outcome needs its own validation. Faster traversal of
an incumbent-created burden is not evidence that the burden was removed.

### Counterexample-to-construction repair

Use admitted counterexamples to identify a supported failure family. Excavate
only when the enabling mechanism is unclear; compare organizations when structure
is live; derive a construction and preserve required-valid behavior. Execute only
under the target's authority, then require its existing family-exclusion proof.
Do not turn each counterexample into another fallback, validator, or branch.

### Ablative normalization

Identify independent factors and their live obligations, remove or quotient
unearned factors, and recompose a canonical form under a selected preservation
relation. Deletion and normalization are related but not identical. Do not demand
strict isomorphism when intentional, authorized removal requires refinement.

### Anticeremonial execution

Bind to the object-level end, remove only process without a distinct causal
obligation, and use the direct legal route. Preserve coordination, safety,
authority, and proof. Do not create a ceremony ledger to justify deleting one.

### Critical adjudication

Treat a review comment as a claim, not authority. Examine counterevidence;
preserve genuine underdetermination only until the decisive distinction or an
honest obstruction is exposed. Issue a criteria-backed disposition when evidence
permits it. Do not keep the question open merely to appear rigorous.

## Collisions to resolve

- **Aporetic / dispositive:** preserve the unresolved question first; stop when
  determining evidence is established. Neither justifies indefinite rumination
  or confidence-based waiver of a required challenger.
- **Retopologizing / saltatory:** the former changes adjacency; the latter is a
  desired discontinuity. Reaching the same distant option faster is not an
  adjacency change, and changing adjacency does not prove a breakthrough.
- **Audacious / pusillanimity:** one expands candidates; the other names a failure
  of conception. Neither authorizes larger scope, unsupported claims, or a bias
  against a small correct patch.
- **Poietic / recombinative:** creating a form and recombining existing primitives
  make different claims. Recombination may produce a new form, but the result must
  still be concrete and evaluable.
- **Retopologizing / architectonic:** search changes which organizations are
  considered; the authorized selector decides which organization governs.
- **Derivational / actuating:** one explains construction from the contract; the
  other performs a legal state transition. A derivation is not execution evidence.
- **Ablative / preservation relation:** deletion is the operation; isomorphism,
  bisimulation, observational equivalence, or refinement states what must survive.
- **Anticeremonial / no process:** remove only unowned process; do not delete tests,
  review, permission checks, or evidence just because they are procedural.
- **Parsimony / smallness of conception:** minimize sufficient realization, not
  the governing objective. A large framework can still avoid the decisive move.

refresh current subject or proof evidence. Add no invocation receipt.

## Reject a composition when

Its operators are synonyms, multiple expansion steps have no selector or exit,
aporia has no re-entry, a candidate selects itself by ambition, a proof relation
is used as the mutation, or a context reset discards established facts. Reject
rewrites that collapse authority, privacy, safety, type, or information-flow
boundaries, and any stack whose only visible effect is stronger vocabulary.

The native procedure must end in a material route delta, an evaluable candidate,
a selected construction, authorized state movement, proof or counterexample,
incumbent retention, no change, or a concrete obstruction. More analysis,
confidence, options, and narration are not terminal value by themselves.
