# Cognitive Operator Basis

Consult while Tune authors a cognitive instruction and needs to distinguish
candidate operations, specialist companions, or proof relations. This is
conditional design knowledge, not a runtime dispatcher or vocabulary checklist.
The names below are local authoring shorthand; express the chosen behavior in
the target skill's language.

## Type grammar

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| Context binder | Identifies the exact state, subject, or authority to which reasoning applies |
| Transform | Changes framing, search, representation, construction, reduction, or action |
| Governor | Constrains how another operation proceeds or stops |
| Selector | Chooses among admissible candidates under declared criteria |
| Construction method | Derives or realizes executable structure from a contract |
| Proof relation | States what must be preserved or equivalent |
| Outcome predicate | Characterizes the state sought or reached |
| Failure predicate | Names a condition to detect or reject |

Do not compose outcomes, failures, relations, and operations as interchangeable
imperatives. Read [cognitive-composition.md](cognitive-composition.md) before
combining operations; no target needs every role as an additional stage.

## Context binders

### SITUATED

Bind reasoning to the live repository, worktree, branch, tools, permissions,
artifacts, and evidence. Trigger when generic or remembered state is being
mistaken for the current operating context. Distinguish observation from
inference; stop when that context and its invalidators are established.
Shadow: an inventory that changes no decision.

### SUBJECT-INDEXED

Bind observations, reviews, proofs, and credit to the exact subject state in
scope. Trigger when evidence may belong to another head, scope, or state;
unmatched evidence loses applicability. Stop when subject identity and the
material-change rule are explicit. Shadow: hashing an inadequately defined
subject and treating the hash as semantic coverage.

### REBASELINING

Discard stale assumptions and bind the decision to current authoritative state.
Trigger when requirements, evidence, authority, head, or environment changes;
retain, invalidate, or supersede prior artifacts according to applicability.
Stop when the current baseline and next legal action are known. Shadow:
deleting still-valid knowledge merely because time passed or a label changed.

## Transforms

### DEFAMILIARIZING

Make inherited assumptions, accidental conventions, and normalized anomalies
visible again. Trigger when familiarity suppresses evidence or alternatives.
Suspend inherited conclusions as authority while preserving verified facts.
Stop after a material assumption, anomaly, or alternative frame becomes visible,
or the incumbent survives. Shadow: novelty bias and needless rediscovery.
"Use fresh eyes" can name this operation but is not its behavioral specification.

### EXCAVATORY

Descend below symptoms or the first explanation until the next layer changes the
mechanism, owner, invariant, intervention, or proof burden. Trace causal,
representational, historical, and ownership layers only as relevant to that
question. Stop when another layer would no longer change the explanation or
route. Shadow: verbosity, speculative archaeology, or endless causal regress.

### RETOPOLOGIZING

Change what counts as a neighboring concept, component, observation, or move so
previously distant candidates become reachable and false inherited neighbors
cease to govern search. Trigger when repository, organizational, or
representational proximity traps the candidate set. Require a structural warrant:
a shared invariant, observation, translation, or ownership obligation. Stop at a
materially new candidate, removal of a false proximity, or failure of the proposed
rewrite. Shadow: arbitrary association, complete-graph novelty, or collapsed
authority, privacy, type, safety, and information-flow boundaries.

### AUDACIOUS

Admit consequential, high-upside, structurally different candidates suppressed
by excessive caution or deference. Widen the candidate set within the accepted
objective and authority; do not relax validity criteria. Stop when a serious
nonlocal candidate is comparable under the same obligations. Shadow: scope
inflation, grandiosity, or confidence substituted for proof. Patch size does not
measure audacity.

### POIETIC

Construct a new representation, mechanism, abstraction, artifact, or strategy
when inherited options contain no adequate form. Produce something concrete with
an evaluation path; stop when it is specific enough to adjudicate or realize.
Shadow: decorative novelty or imaginative prose without construction.
"Operate poietically" does not exempt the result from the existing selector.

### RECOMBINATIVE

Compose existing primitives into a candidate when their combination can satisfy
a missing capability. Preserve the primitives' contracts and check their
interaction. Stop when the combination is evaluable. Recombination may create a
new form, but invoking familiar parts is not itself evidence of poiesis.
Shadow: analogy without a lawful composition or an evaluation path.

### ABLATIVE

Remove surface without a distinct live obligation: duplicated truth, dominated
paths, expired scaffolding, compatibility residue, or dilution. Delete, collapse,
quotient, privatize, canonicalize, or decommission under a selected preservation
relation. Stop when every retained factor owns a live obligation and every removed
factor is accounted for. Shadow: deleting unfamiliar or old code without proving
what its removal preserves.

### ACTUATING

Find the controlling seam, apply the dominant legal lever, and verify state
movement. Trigger when a selected, authorized route stays at analysis or planning.
Name target, blocker, control point, action, and proof; stop at verified movement
or a concrete obstruction. Shadow: activity mistaken for progress, premature
action, or mutation during inspection. This label is not a replacement for the
`$actuating` skill or its construction and closure contract.

### REIFYING

Turn hidden behavior into explicit data that can be inspected, routed, compared,
stored, serialized, or interpreted. Trigger when callbacks, closures, implicit
control, or effects obstruct a required operation. Define the admitted behavior
algebra and its interpreter; require total interpretation over admitted
constructors or expose the unsupported boundary. Stop when representation and
preservation obligations are explicit. Shadow: extra data structures that leave
the actual behavior space open or unvalidated.

## Governors

### APORETIC

Preserve genuine contradiction or underdetermination instead of forcing closure.
Name the incompatible credible claims, missing distinction, decisive evidence,
and adjudicative re-entry. Stop when evidence resolves the difficulty, a reversible
decision is justified, or an obstruction is reported. Shadow: indecision,
ambiguity theater, or rumination after the determining evidence is available.

### ANTICEREMONIAL

Reject process whose visible performance displaces object-level capability.
For a plan, gate, ledger, handoff, or narration surface, ask what correctness,
authority, safety, coordination, or proof property would be lost on removal.
Stop when each retained element owns a distinct live obligation. Shadow: removing
necessary protections merely because they are procedural.

### CALIBRATED

Match confidence, claims, and action to evidence and consequence. Trigger when
certainty exceeds support; state bounds, uncertainty, and overturn conditions.
Stop when claim strength is proportionate. Shadow: timidity disguised as humility.

### PARSIMONIOUS

Prefer the minimum sufficient assumptions, concepts, and independent semantic
commitments among valid formulations. Stop when no weaker sufficient formulation
remains. Shadow: a tiny edit that retains the wrong abstraction or adds a broad
restriction. Economy of realization is not smallness of conception.

## Selectors

### ARCHITECTONIC

Compare organizing abstractions, boundaries, owners, and composition laws when
the decomposition itself is suspect. Evaluate live obligations, required
observations, canonical ownership, lawful composition, conceptual compression,
and proof. Stop at dominance, honest incomparability, or obstruction. Shadow:
architecture theater and abstraction proliferation. The target's authorized
architecture selector owns the decision; Tune only authors its instruction.

### ADJUDICATIVE

Issue a criteria-backed disposition among competing claims or candidates.
Declare criteria before rewarding an outcome; select, retain, specialize, defer,
reject, or obstruct. Stop with the disposition and overturn condition. Shadow:
preference laundered into judgment.

### DISPOSITIVE

Identify what actually determines a decision and stop once it is established.
Separate material evidence from merely interesting considerations. Shadow:
premature closure around a plausible fact. Confidence alone cannot waive a
challenge required by the target's contract.

### PRINCIPAL

Seek the least-committed adequate general solution from which required cases
follow by instantiation. Trigger when special cases may share one lawful
construction. Stop when every required case is an instance or justified exception.
Shadow: generality that weakens the actual contract or conceals incomparability.

## Construction methods

### AXIOMATIC

Separate observations, constraints, chosen policies, and assumptions; expose the
smallest defensible premise set and derive forward rather than inheriting the
current implementation. Stop when premises are sufficient, underdetermined,
inconsistent, or blocked. Shadow: treating a convenient assumption as accepted
source authority.

### INTRINSIC

Put correctness into representations, constructors, types, and legal compositions
rather than relying only on post-hoc discipline. Trigger when forbidden states or
transitions remain representable. Stop when they are excluded where the host
permits and residual extrinsic obligations are explicit. Shadow: claiming that
types prove properties the encoding never enforces.

### DERIVATIONAL

Obtain executable structure from the accepted contract through preserving
construction steps. Give each step its refinement or proof obligation and treat
the final program as a witness relative to declared premises. Shadow: calling
implementation-first plus tests a derivation or hiding unproved assumptions.

### COUNTEREXAMPLE-GUIDED

Use failures to refine an abstraction, invariant, or admitted domain instead of
accumulating symptom-specific patches. Trigger when each failure adds another
local guard. Stop when the supported family is structurally explained or remains
explicitly open. Shadow: overgeneralizing from sampled failures or excluding
required-valid behavior to make the tests pass.

## Proof relations

A relation constrains a transformation; it does not perform one. State what may
intentionally disappear before selecting the relation.

| Relation | Obligation | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| ISOMORPHIC | Invertible structural correspondence | Strict structure-preserving change |
| BISIMULATIVE | Mutually matching observable transitions | State machines, protocols, interpreters |
| OBSERVATIONALLY-EQUIVALENT | Equality under declared observations | Representation changes with hidden internals |
| REFINEMENT-PRESERVING | Required behavior survives while explicitly unrequired behavior may disappear | Ablation and contract-strengthening |
| CONGRUENCE-PRESERVING | Equivalence survives every permitted context | Quotienting and rewrite systems |

Do not use isomorphism as a synonym for deletion or general simplification.

## Outcome predicates

These describe goals or closure properties, not standalone implementation methods.

| Predicate | Required account | Characteristic failure |
|---|---|---|
| SALTATORY | Incumbent frontier, limiting assumption, discontinuity, newly reachable state, validation path | Novelty, magnitude, or confidence mislabeled as breakthrough |
| CONFLUENT | Critical pairs, join states, canonical normal form | Assuming distinct valid paths necessarily join |
| FIXED-POINT | Observation surface, absence of further material change, reopen condition | Repeated clean-looking passes mistaken for closure |
| CONTRACTIVE | Named distance to closure that each iteration strictly reduces | Iteration without a valid measure or reroute for non-contraction |
| COMPLETE | Every semantically valid case in the declared domain | Confusing completeness with soundness, totality, decidability, or sampled coverage |

## Failure predicates

These are conditions to detect, not additional workflow stages.

**PUSILLANIMITY:** shrinking the objective or conception beneath the governing
stakes to avoid ambition, disagreement, responsibility, or difficult ownership.
Distinguish it from parsimony (economy of realization), surgicality (narrowness of
intervention), and calibration (claims matched to evidence). A one-line fix can
embody the governing move; a large framework can avoid it.

**DILUTION:** accumulated surface whose marginal capability does not justify its
semantic commitments, proof burden, ownership cost, maintenance, or change cost.

**CEREMONY:** process that performs diligence without causally owning a live
object-level obligation. Retain process that does own one.

**UNSOUNDNESS:** a guarantee, derivation, abstraction, or route that admits a
counterexample under its stated assumptions.
