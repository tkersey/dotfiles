# Cognitive Compilation

Use within Tune when authoring or repairing how a target agent reasons at a
consequential decision point. This is not a runtime service, public mode, or
final-prose pass. Tune owns diagnosis, intervention selection, package mutation,
validation, and publication; the target skill retains its domain decisions and
operational effects.

## Working basis

Reuse Tune's existing expected delta and protected contract. Identify the target
skill, owned decision, incumbent instruction or missing behavior, requested or
observed weakness, non-triggers, authority boundaries, and observable success and
failure. Do not create a second input packet or reconstruct the same evidence.

Current source plus a clear requested change can suffice for create or direct
edit work. Claims about observed behavior, recurrence, or causality still need
behavioral evidence. Do not turn a requested capability into an invented history
of failures. Stop with Tune's `blocked` route when missing target, protected
behavior, or authority makes the proposed policy change unsafe to bound.

```text
requested cognitive improvement or witnessed weakness
  -> target-owned decision point
  -> smallest sufficient reasoning operation
  -> native trigger, operation, guard, and stop
  -> observable behavioral difference
  -> discriminating probes
```

## Select by pressure, not vocabulary

The operator names are authoring shorthand, not stages to install or mandatory
words in the target. Ordinary native wording is preferable when sufficient.

| Pressure at the target's decision point | Candidate operation | Required difference |
|---|---|---|
| Inherited framing hides current evidence | Defamiliarize | Preserve facts while reopening an unsupported frame |
| Proof or premises belong to another subject | Rebaseline or bind the subject | Restore evidence applicability, not redesign by default |
| Diagnosis stops at symptoms | Excavate | Reach the mechanism, owner, invariant, or proof burden that changes the repair |
| Credible constraints remain incompatible | Preserve the aporia | Expose the missing distinction and an adjudicative re-entry |
| Search stays inside incumbent adjacency | Retopologize | Admit a structurally warranted new candidate neighborhood |
| Caution suppresses a consequential admissible candidate | Widen candidate generation | Compare the candidate without relaxing evidence or authority |
| No available form satisfies the objective | Construct a new form | Produce a concrete representation or mechanism with an evaluation path |
| Decomposition or ownership is suspect | Compare organizing structures | Put candidates through the target's architecture selector |
| Implementation is detached from accepted laws | Derive; encode invariants intrinsically where possible | Connect executable structure to the contract and residual proof |
| Accumulated surface has no distinct live obligation | Ablate | Delete or consolidate under a named preservation relation |
| Process displaces object-level capability | Remove ceremony | Retain necessary safety, authority, coordination, and proof |
| A selected legal route never becomes action | Actuate | Require verified state movement, not more recommendations |

Consult [cognitive-operators.md](cognitive-operators.md) only when choosing among
operations or when a specialist distinction matters. Read
[cognitive-composition.md](cognitive-composition.md) before combining distinct
operations. Do not enumerate the catalog for every edit or prefer a larger stack
because its words sound stronger.

## Compile at the native decision point

1. **Locate the decision.** Replace "be more creative" with the actual question,
   such as whether synchronization is necessary or a derived projection would
   preserve the same observations. No material decision, candidate, construction,
   deletion, action, or proof difference means no cognitive intervention.
2. **Choose the smallest sufficient operation.** Check for an equivalent native
   handler first. Retain it, sharpen only the witnessed ambiguity, or choose
   `no-change`. A direct clarification that achieves the delta with fewer semantic
   commitments dominates an elaborate cognitive procedure.
3. **Write in the target's language.** Name its real owners, artifacts,
   invariants, and dispositions. Replace superseded generic prose instead of
   adding a doctrine paragraph beside the operative rule. An evocative phrase may
   remain when useful, but does not substitute for the behavioral instruction.
4. **Bind a narrow trigger.** Prefer an observed mismatch, recurrence, or
   representation burden over "on difficult tasks." Preserve valid near misses.
   Do not broaden implicit activation merely to make the new instruction fire.
5. **Guard the characteristic failure.** Boldness widens search, not permission;
   retopology requires structural warrant; aporia requires re-entry; ablation
   needs obligation accounting and a preservation relation. Keep authority,
   safety, privacy, type, information-flow, compatibility, and proof boundaries.
6. **Give the operation an exit.** Stop when the next causal layer would not
   change the route, a new candidate is ready for comparison, the missing
   distinction resolves the question, or a concrete obstruction is known. The
   incumbent may survive. Add no duplicate pass over an unchanged comparison.
7. **State the observable difference.** A changed owner, candidate family,
   representation, deletion decision, executable step, or proof obligation can
   qualify. A rejected challenger qualifies only when it changes the evidential
   basis, such as exposing an assumption or a decisive discriminator. Adoption,
   participation, stronger language, and higher confidence do not establish value.
8. **Use existing selection and validation.** Return the proposed native rule to
   Tune's ordinary intervention selection without a handoff. Keep always-required
   authority, triggers, and stops in the target kernel; put conditional detail
   behind an explicit reference. Do not copy this catalog into the target.

Preserve independently owned canonical interventions. In particular,
[Metanoetic's SKILL.md](../../metanoetic/SKILL.md) remains the sole canonical text
for `$metanoetic`. This authoring taxonomy neither rewrites that text nor adds a
prerequisite to explicit invocation or a workflow's native trigger. Compilation
itself does not require executing a Metanoetic pass.

## Native instruction examples

These are adaptations to evaluate against the target's actual contract, not
snippets to paste into every skill or stronger substitutes for existing rules.

### Change candidate locality

Instead of "think outside the box":

```text
Before selecting among incumbent-shaped candidates, test whether repository,
service, representation, or ownership proximity is being mistaken for semantic
adjacency. Admit a new adjacency only when a shared invariant, observation,
translation, or ownership obligation warrants it. Route the resulting candidate
through the existing architecture selector. Stop after one material candidate
delta or after the incumbent neighborhood survives the challenge.
```

A new relevance relation never confers access, ownership, or publication rights.

### Trace recurring findings to their enabling structure

Instead of "find the root cause":

```text
When multiple findings touch the same semantic surface, descend from each local
symptom until a shared owner, information-loss mechanism, or governing invariant
explains the supported failure family. Compare a construction that excludes that
family against comment-local repairs, preserving required-valid behavior. Stop
when another causal layer would not change the repair route. Use the existing
repair disposition; do not create an excavation artifact.
```

Do not infer a universal failure family from a single undifferentiated example.

### Remove surface by obligation

Instead of "prefer less code":

```text
Inventory each independent factor and the live obligation it owns. Delete,
collapse, quotient, privatize, or canonicalize factors without a distinct
obligation. Recompose the survivors into a coherent normal form and prove the
selected preservation or refinement relation. Stop when removed factors are
accounted for and each retained factor owns a necessary obligation.
```

A small component can own a critical law; line count is not the selection rule.

### Derive executable structure

Instead of "implement correctly and run tests":

```text
Derive implementation steps from the accepted contract, governing laws,
required observations, and admitted assumptions. Prefer representations and
constructors that exclude invalid states. Keep residual correctness properties
as explicit proof obligations. The final program is an executable witness
relative to those premises; tests provide evidence but do not replace the
construction argument.
```

Do not claim correctness against unstated requirements or unproved assumptions.

### Remove process without removing its protections

Instead of "keep the process lightweight":

```text
For each plan, gate, receipt, handoff, or narration surface, identify the unique
capability, correctness, authority, safety, coordination, or proof obligation it
owns. Inline, collapse, or delete surface whose removal preserves those
properties. Stop when every retained process element has a distinct causal role.
Do not replace deleted ceremony with a new governance layer.
```

The absence of a live obligation must be established, not inferred from dislike
of procedural work.

### Move a selected route into verified action

Instead of "do not stop at analysis":

```text
Once the route is selected and action is authorized, identify the current
blocker, controlling seam, and smallest legal effect that changes system state.
Execute it, verify the movement, and continue from the next bottleneck or report
a concrete obstruction. Commands, edits, and status updates alone are not proof
of progress, and state movement alone is not proof of closure.
```

Do not let this instruction turn inspection or proposal-only work into mutation.

## Probe and evidence discipline

For a proposed cognitive change, use
[cognitive-compilation-probes.md](cognitive-compilation-probes.md) to derive the
smallest useful positive, near-miss, and shadow-failure cases. Adapt existing
probes rather than creating a second suite. Exercise both Tune's authoring
behavior and the target's resulting behavior when a live evaluation is practical.

Compare outcomes and intermediate decision evidence under the same task,
accepted authority, model configuration, and available tools. Historical episodes
can nominate a weakness; fresh matched runs are stronger evidence for an adoption
claim when feasible. An existing `$emulator` workflow may support that comparison,
but is not a mandatory dependency for an ordinary edit.

Distinguish authored probe expectations, structural checks, executed observations,
and causal claims. Report what actually ran and what remains unproved through
Tune's existing report. Do not add a compilation receipt, operator ledger, or
mandatory experiment merely to demonstrate use of this reference.

## Reject unnecessary compilation

Choose `no-change` when an equivalent native rule already suffices, the requested
term changes only style, no narrow trigger or behavioral delta can be named, the
operation duplicates another owner, or the proposal broadens protected authority
or activation. For a mechanical correction, skip this procedure and make the
already-authorized edit; `no-change` for cognition does not cancel that work.

A template is a candidate, not permission to mutate. Inspection remains read-only,
and a selected instruction still passes Tune's semantic-weakness and publication
gates. No claim of increased model intelligence follows from doctrine wording.
