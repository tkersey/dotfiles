# Cybernetic

## Mission

Move from events to the structure that keeps producing them, then select a
feedback-aware leverage point without seizing implementation.

```text
events -> patterns -> structure -> mental models -> leverage -> monitored intervention
```

Do not use this skill for one clear local defect.

## Common path

1. Bind the system boundary and the repeated pattern.
2. Classify the system or its subsystems:
   - `clear` -> known checklist;
   - `complicated` -> expert analysis;
   - `complex` -> safe-to-fail probes and adaptation;
   - `chaotic` -> stabilize first;
   - `mixed` -> split by subsystem.
3. Use DART:
   - **Deconstruct** parts, stocks, flows, owners, and boundaries;
   - **Analyze** causal structure, delays, and feedback;
   - **Recognize** recurring archetypes and incentives;
   - **Test** the smallest safe probe or stabilizing action.
4. Name reinforcing loops, balancing loops, delayed or distorted feedback, and
   the measured proxy that may be gamed.
5. Compare leverage at the lowest level that can actually change the pattern:
   parameter, buffer, stock/flow structure, delay, feedback, information, rules,
   self-organization, goal, or paradigm.
6. Select one reversible intervention, leading and lagging signals, stop
   conditions, and the downstream owner.
7. Hand implementation back to `$actuating`, `$reduce`, `$universalist`,
   `$review-fold`, `$negative-ledger`, `$tune`, or another exact owner.

Use a compact `cybernetic_context` for companion work. Produce the full packet
only when system structure itself is the primary deliverable.

## Guardrails

- Do not mistake an event for the system.
- Do not apply a clear-system checklist to a complex system.
- Do not analyze indefinitely in chaos; stabilize first.
- Do not optimize a part while degrading the whole.
- Do not change rules, goals, or paradigms without mapping power, incentives,
  costs, delays, and second-order effects.
- Prefer no action or observation when the feedback model is unsupported.
