# Semantic Hotspots

Load when a witnessed state or trace implicates construction, transition,
composition, ownership, representation, admission, or observation. A hotspot is
source-supported causal pressure, not a bug count or a durable classification.

Use [counterexample-guided-normalization.md](counterexample-guided-normalization.md)
for the current and retained evidence, accepted law, required-valid observations,
and preselected discriminator. Locate the first loss of guarantee: the independently
chosen value, write, ordering, or authority that permits the failure. For temporal
or aliasing pressure, start from admitted valid state and try the implicated
operation. Do not invent a family or a new abstraction for a mechanical defect.

Derive all relevant sanctioned construction and operation paths from repository
sources. Native evidence may establish coverage directly; explicit topology is
needed when route or migration accounting is otherwise unestablished. Neither a
cut crossing nor a checked constructor proves that later operations preserve the
law. Required observations, progress, and legitimate guards must survive.

When the ordinary explanation is enumerative, detection-shaped, contradicted,
or incumbent-captive, use the same Glaze-primed Metanoetic pass defined in
[architecture-reconciliation.md](architecture-reconciliation.md), under the
unchanged Metanoetic trigger; do not run a second pass. Actuating adjudicates the
challenger and any changed causal explanation in its existing selection decision.
A live boundary decision then receives a compact Universalist nomination; it does
not require an additional theory protocol or another Working Set projection.

A current same-claim witness revokes exclusion. A source-domain omission revokes
coverage. Reopen the affected explanation, not every implementation detail.
Local correction and representation change compete under the same authority,
family, required-valid, preservation, and coverage obligations. Unknown authority
or applicability cannot authorize correctness work; samples cannot prove universal
exclusion. A conservative abstraction may exclude optional safe behavior, never
required-valid behavior or observations.

Return only the material operation-level cause, construction pressure,
discriminator, evidence, and unresolved limitation not already carried by Actuating.
No durable hotspot, route certificate, duplicate report, or repair packet.
