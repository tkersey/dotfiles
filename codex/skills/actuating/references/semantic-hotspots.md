# Semantic Hotspots

Normative only when a witnessed incorrect state or trace may expose a defect in
construction, transition, composition, ownership, representation,
interpretation, or observation.

## Disclosure contract

**Load when:** a current bug, finding, incident, migration failure, or repeated
same-law defense touches a semantic boundary.

**Do not load when:** one isolated implementation mistake occurs inside an
already-correct owner and no prior elimination claim has been falsified.

**Return:** one bounded counterexample theory, topology, proof posture, and
reconsideration falsifier. Create no durable artifact.

## From witness to theory

A bug is one witness, not the family.

```text
Witness provenance
  exact version/build/environment/input/state/trace observed

Current applicability
  still-present | transformed-applicable | already-excluded |
  not-comparable | unknown

Law authority
  entailed | strengthening | preference | new-requirement | underdetermined

Detection surface
  where incorrectness became visible

Counterexample theory
  Theta = (Omega, Alpha, L, Phi, A, O, C, Vh, Q)
```

Only currently applicable `entailed` evidence creates automatic pressure on the
current Goal. Other authority classes are follow-up, rejection, Goal reopening,
or unresolved authority—not current counterexamples.

## Sound abstraction

For candidate `K`:

```text
U_K
  concrete behaviors considered at sanctioned admission surfaces

B_K subset U_K
  behaviors actually admitted

alpha_K : U_K -> Omega
  candidate interpretation

Bad_L(u)
  concrete behavior u violates law L
```

Safety requires:

```text
Totality
  alpha_K(u) is defined for every u in U_K

Violation reflection
  Bad_L(u) -> Phi(alpha_K(u))

Abstract exclusion
  b in B_K -> not Phi(alpha_K(b))

Goal adequacy
  every required valid behavior remains admitted and every required
  observation retains its accepted meaning
```

Diagnostic exactness is separate:

```text
Phi(alpha_K(u)) -> Bad_L(u)
```

A conservative overapproximation may reject optional safe behavior. It does not
leave residual incorrectness unless it removes accepted valid behavior or
required meaning.

The architectonic objective is the least discriminating sound abstraction:
forget accidental representation, never concrete danger or required meaning.

## Generative family

A family is not a list of observed examples. State:

```text
semantic domain Omega
law L and provenance
predicate or generator Phi
sanctioned admission semantics A
owner model O
frontier or minimal admission cut C
claim strength Q
family-theory falsifier
```

Then state either an exhaustive finite-domain argument or predicted sibling
counterexamples. A useful theory predicts unobserved members by law and
generator, identifies the paths that could admit them, and supplies probes that
try to falsify the intended exclusion.

If the theory only restates observed examples, it remains `hypothesized`,
`sampled`, `contained`, or `unresolved`.

## Metanoetic challenge

Invoke `$metanoetic` once on one unchanged decision surface when the ordinary
theory is:

- detection-shaped;
- witness-enumerating;
- representation-bound;
- violation-erasing;
- contradicted by a witness that falsifies the family claim inside its validity
  horizon;
- captive to the incumbent owner or topology; or
- one of several materially different live explanations.

The challenger may revise `Omega`, `Alpha`, `L`, `Phi`, `A`, `O`, `C`, or `Q`.
Metanoetic generates; Actuating adjudicates.

## OPERATE ARCHITECTONICALLY

Compare ordinary and challenger theories by:

```text
law provenance and witness coverage
soundness and Goal adequacy
causal compression and sibling prediction
canonical ownership
lawful construction, elimination, and composition
admission-cut coverage and escape paths
proof strength and falsifiers
reconstitution and operational cost
```

Return:

```text
retain | replace | combine | split | unresolved
```

Do not select the most abstract vocabulary. Select the smallest coherent
organization that makes the law structural.

## Semantic hotspot

A hotspot is the ephemeral conjunction:

```text
law
x predicate-defined family
x owner set
x frontier/cut
x escape paths
x independent witnesses
```

It is not a file, churn score, review count, bug count, or persistent record.

## Realization defect or architecture defect

Preserve architecture only when the incumbent already has:

- canonical semantic authority;
- an adequate representation and state space;
- complete sanctioned-path coverage;
- a sound interpretation;
- a mechanism capable of excluding the family; and
- no live sanctioned escape path requiring a new mechanism.

Reopen architecture when a witness exposes constructible forbidden state,
illegal transition/composition, missing or distributed authority, bypassable
enforcement, a missing semantic law, compensating guard accretion, or a theory
that cannot predict or reflect the witness.

## Post-elimination witness

When a current `entailed` witness falsifies an eliminated family inside its
validity horizon, stop this module's normal repair path and read
[post-elimination-falsification.md](post-elimination-falsification.md).

That witness revokes the prior elimination lease. It must be localized to a
failed theory, interpretation, admission, ownership, realization, proof, or
claim-strength premise before another mutation.

## Proof

A first or successor `eliminated` claim requires:

```text
current-witness rejection
sound interpretation
Goal adequacy
family-level abstract exclusion
frontier/cut and bypass coverage
exhaustive proof or sibling predictions plus probes
honest residual ownership
claim strength no stronger than the weakest evidence
```

Passing repaired examples alone is not family exclusion.

## Hard exclusions

- No bug Ledger, hotspot registry/database, score, threshold, receipt family,
  predecessor chain, or durable Actuating state.
- Do not define a family as only observed examples.
- Do not let review prose silently create a new Goal law.
- Do not preserve elimination after a current entailed witness falsifies its
  exact family claim inside the validity horizon.
- Do not patch under a revoked elimination lease.
- Do not call newly discovered siblings prior predictions.
- Do not conflate detection with admission, conservative restriction with
  residual incorrectness, or review repetition with convergence.
