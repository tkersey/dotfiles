# Construction arguments and behavioral evaluation

Use when [initial construction](../SKILL.md#initial-construction) or
[counterexample-driven selection](../SKILL.md#compile-the-first-loss-of-guarantee)
needs a domain, operation, or migration argument. The accepted Goal supports initial
construction without a witness or predecessor theorem. Actuating owns construction
selection; [Review Fold](../../review-fold/SKILL.md#counterexample-admission) owns
witness admission and applicability. Reuse their evidence, not a new decision procedure.

For a supported family, check `required-valid intersect Phi = empty`: revise a
conflicting hypothesis or seek authority, never silently reject required-valid
behavior. An absent corpus limits historical claims; it neither proves absence nor
prevents initial implementation. Preserve witness provenance when stronger proof
subsumes tests.

## Constructive derivation

Use the [initial-construction derivation](../SKILL.md#initial-construction): compare
what the next required observation needs with what the code and authority establish.
Let the unsupported implication choose a native construction or deciding inspection.
The moves below and the [owner examples](#derive-the-missing-premise-at-its-owner)
are alternatives selected by that uncertainty, not a sequence, quota, or prerequisite
for an adequate direct fix:

| Law or premise to establish | Native construction to derive | Deciding countercase |
|---|---|---|
| A value is determined by another fact. | Derive it through that fact's owner instead of admitting another independent setting. | Equal current values carry distinct required history or admit different future behavior. |
| Only certain states or operation sequences are valid. | Tagged cases and owner-controlled transitions, with the information each case requires. | An alias revives a consumed state, or the protocol forbids a required retry. |
| An algorithm must achieve a postcondition and progress. | Work backward into an invariant, a preserving step, and a well-founded decrease or other contract-appropriate progress argument; choose state that supports them. | The invariant holds while execution stalls, overflows, or skips a required result. |
| Meaning is determined by recursive cases and their combination. | A native fold or transition interpreter with justified primitive meanings and composition. | Cases agree separately but combination duplicates an input, mishandles the empty case, or reorders an effect. |
| A judgment must survive a phase or representation change. | Relate the representations through authoritative facts and preserving primitive rules; consumers instantiate rather than reconstruct their meaning. | Both phases pass local checks while a required distinction, accepted state, or subsequent observation is lost. |
| An intermediate exists only to supply an observation. | Compute that observation directly, fuse a traversal, or scope production to consumption. | Inspection, identity, sharing, replay, early termination, effects, or resource behavior requires the discarded structure. |
| Complex search produces a compact checkable witness. | An untrusted producer followed by a small, independently justified checker before trusted admission. | Producer and checker share a false premise; sound rejection alone does not establish required search completeness or progress. |

Establish primitive meaning and why permitted composition preserves it. Choose
the relation required by the source: exact observations or round trips when
promised, otherwise an adequate refinement or simulation. Refinement may choose
among merely permitted nondeterministic outcomes; it must preserve required
choices, observations, continuations, and progress. Difficulty proving a step,
recovering an observation, or expressing lawful composition is feedback to revise
the representation, algorithm, or interpretation, not merely add predicates.

For an unresolved operation vocabulary, interacting law family, or observation
carrier at a live boundary, reuse Universalist's
[domain-algebra derivation](../../universalist/references/domain-algebra/algebra-driven-design.md).
Use the existing decision and common proof obligations; no new pass, IR, framework,
packet, or second implementation is implied.

## Construction argument and source-derived coverage

A candidate that changes the comparison domain, quotients distinctions, or
conservatively approximates behavior must justify total interpretation, violation
reflection, exclusion, and required-valid observation preservation. Justify safe
rejection and required-valid acceptance separately: an overapproximation can impose
obligations that invalidate required recovery. Preserve enough precision for the
source-required judgments, not exact whole-program inference by default. Optional
safe behavior excluded by approximation must be disclosed; no required distinction
may be erased. Do not invent these transformations for candidates that do not use them.

Use the strongest adequate native evidence for the affected seam: closed types
and their construction/operation surface, compiler IR, schemas, exports, build or
route registries, reachability, or generated producer/codec inventories. Bind the
exact subject, domain, assumptions, strength, and falsifier. A candidate-authored
path list cannot establish its own completeness. Unknown dynamic paths require
an explicit bound or residual, not silent omission.

Derive required semantic content separately from path coverage: which facts,
records, relationships, and observations must exist under the accepted law?
Identify the retained facts, transitions, or commitments that generate those
obligations and establish their completeness for the declared domain. Checking
all supplied members, or calling their collection canonical, cannot establish
required presence. If an obligation and its evidence can both disappear without
any surviving distinction, admission cannot prove historical completeness. Use
source-permitted retained evidence or expose that limitation; an unmet required
law remains unresolved. Reference reachability, semantic agreement, and required
chronology or progress are separate obligations.

For an invariant-style safety law, establish that admitted starting states satisfy
the invariant, every permitted observable transition preserves it, and the invariant
implies the law. Check ownership/lifetime and sanctioned escapes. An intrinsic
carrier, admitted refinement, or encapsulated transition can enforce the law;
its name cannot. Transient invalidity must remain unobservable outside its owner.
Unique identity or an explicit equivalence must be respected by trusted operations.
Required progress, ordering, external effects, and other trace obligations are not
proved merely by a state invariant or by refusing every operation.

A closed native surface may discharge coverage directly: establish all public
construction and operation paths, lawful transitions, and the absence of writable
aliases or unchecked trusted mints. Include codecs, recovery, compatibility, unsafe
operations, and foreign interfaces when present. Opacity or a successful build
alone does not establish those facts. Reuse an adequate native proof rather than
redescribing it as a graph, transformation, and disposition table.

When a predecessor needs accounting and native evidence does not otherwise
establish route or migration coverage, use explicit topology reconciliation.
Derive predecessor `T0` from source, give
Universalist that domain with one live axis and typed hole, and select the verifier
before implementation. On the actual successor derive `T1` independently of the
candidate's asserted list and establish:

```text
T1 = tau(T0)                         predecessor-to-successor accounting
domain(F) = T1                      one disposition per actual successor element
every factorized trusted route crosses the law-preserving cut K
retirements and privatizations declared by tau hold in the actual surface
adapters delegate to the owner instead of reimplementing its law
residuals have owners, failure behavior, invalidators, and claim consequences
```

Account for retired predecessors through `tau`, not by requiring them in `T1`.
Use a graph or generated relation, not enumeration of all execution traces. Cut
coverage establishes passage through the owner; it does not prove preservation
afterward. An admitted value with a mutable external alias can pass the cut and
still violate the invariant at consumption.

Both proof representations must cover every relevant sanctioned path, migration,
retirement, and residual. Newly unaccounted source elements invalidate coverage;
the verifier must not accept a candidate-authored list as its verification domain.
A missing path revokes the coverage claim, and appending its name to a list does
not re-establish completeness. An owned residual still admitting the family
precludes complete elimination of that family.

Finite domains need exhaustive coverage or adequate proof. Complete open-domain
claims need a justified generator and preservation argument. Otherwise state the
bounded domain and residuals. A generated sample or repaired sibling is not a
universal proof. Choose and execute the native verifier on the actual candidate;
a proposed argument or nomination is not executed evidence.

## Producer migration and compensator retirement

Each affected predecessor element is migrated through the owner, retired, privatized,
turned into a derived adapter, or explicitly residual. Prove required-valid
observational preservation, including identity, ordering, custody, errors,
serialization, and composition. Where compatibility is reversible, prove its
round trips modulo the declared equivalence.

After realization, inspect current correctness-bearing factors and retire those
subsumed by the selected construction. A retained factor must carry a distinct
accepted obligation or a justified derived defense-in-depth/observation role.
A source digest, guard count, or smaller diff is not evidence of adequacy.
Preserve witness provenance when redundant tests collapse into a stronger proof;
do not delete an independent oracle merely because it checks the same law.

## Common candidate proof

Apply the [common proof obligations](../SKILL.md#realization-and-common-proof-obligations)
on the actual exact head. If implementation exposes an unconsidered owner, carrier,
interpretation, proof-domain, or topology change, reopen that affected decision and
satisfy its obligations before review or Ship. Neither relabeling the delta nor
correcting one obligation authorizes unrelated strengthening or claim narrowing.

## Worked cases

These illustrate domain selection and derivation, not required implementation patterns.

| Tempting local construction | Source-required domain and adequate construction | Discriminating case |
|---|---|---|
| Check every supplied acquisition attempt has a capture. | Derive acquisitions from retained occurrence semantics whose completeness is established for the claim. | Omit attempt and capture together; the obligation survives. Legitimate occurrences requiring neither remain admissible. |
| Free scratch inside the reported artifact method. | The public call owns independent temporary storage, deep-copies returned values into response storage, and releases scratch before the next batch member. | Completed and failed decoding through another method releases scratch while required results survive. |

### Derive the missing premise at its owner

These historical Protean changes illustrate different adequate responses, not a
requirement to add an owner, fence, or registry to unrelated work.

**Postcommit cancellation.** A durable receipt does not establish that its required
interruption has taken effect. After cancellation commits, a later error must not
leave affected recovery dispatchable without that interruption. Before the commit,
failed admission must retain its rollback and retry behavior. Protean's existing
fail-stop owner and restart recovery made this scoped repair sufficient for the
reported gap ([3444a70](https://github.com/tkersey/protean/commit/3444a70ab2ffd4750bbf66d26cb97ea9f33bdaa9)):

```zig
try self.persist(value, previous, "task.cancel");
errdefer self.store().fence();
```

The placement derives from the phase-dependent consequence, not a list of error
names. Subsequent error returns through that scope invoke the fence, including a
new fallible step that propagates its error there. Swallowed errors, other scopes,
and process crashes are not covered by this rule; justify their required behavior
separately. The source test varies allocation failure across publication, checks
precommit retry and postcommit fencing, then reopens and checks receipt replay.
It challenges the missing implication while the durable receipt still exists.
A small correction is constructive when the native rule supplies the generality.

**Validation before projection.** A read snapshot alone does not establish reusable
retention-validation facts. After import, callers could reach projection without
those facts; reopening happened to restore them but was not required by the API.
Protean gave `Store.project` the registry needed to establish missing cold facts
before sealed projection ([b68778a](https://github.com/tkersey/protean/commit/b68778a41a9051319d6359cdaacad8b521c7096d)).
The condition binds those facts to transaction state, sealed generation, integrity
epoch, and application identity. Transaction-scoped import/audit views still
validate without minting reusable committed-state facts. Snapshot lifetime and
invalidation remain obligations; a label saying "validated" cannot replace them.

The added input supplies legitimate means; it removes the caller's separate duty
to recognize and repair a stale prerequisite. Supplying a registry is not the same
burden as remembering a warm-up sequence. Challenge same-service continuation
through import and repeated projection, observing required output and actual cache
reuse. Zero ancestry bytes alone cannot distinguish reuse from bypassing the cache.

Both examples select code from the missing premise while retaining different phase
semantics and independent checks. Their source tests are inspectable evidence, not
native runs performed by reading this guide or untouched evidence of skill efficacy.

### Derive complete frame representability

Suppose admitted results must remain retrievable under byte, token, and depth
limits. Derive accounting from the encoded structure: for a compact array, bytes
are two brackets, separators, and the sum of encoded child bytes. Strings count
after escaping; tokens and nesting follow the peer's actual definitions. A frame
builder composes these quantities with checked arithmetic and reserves wrapper
and batch overhead. The primitive bounds and their composition establish the
whole-frame bound; separate per-value limits cannot.

To preserve result availability, derive a projection that selects inline output
when it fits, or an exact retrievable reference when the source permits it. The
reference and its wrapper must also fit, and retrieval must make the required
progress within its own bounds. Two individually fitting children, an escaping
string, and an oversized referenced result distinguish these obligations. A final
guard that only rejects oversized output establishes neither representability nor
required retrieval.

### Derive recursive codec preservation

Suppose the source requires `decode(encode(v)) ~= v` for supported values. Begin
with required distinctions: absent field, present null, empty collection, and a
number versus its textual spelling cannot share an encoding if observations
distinguish them. Give the primitive cases adequate encodings, then derive record
and sequence codecs by composing those operations. Establish the round trip for
each primitive and show that the container operations preserve it, including empty
and nested cases. This yields the recursive argument instead of independent
special cases for every consumer.

The reverse byte round trip is required only if the source promises it; permitted
normalization may change spelling while preserving meaning. Mutually agreeing
codecs can still share the wrong convention, so source-distinguishing wire cases
remain independent deciding evidence. When a nested present-null value collapses
to absence, revise the carrier or encoding that lost the distinction.

### Derive judgment preservation across saved computation

Suppose the source requires successfully published checkpoints of admitted execution
to restore and preserve subsequent observations, under the same policy and adequate
resources. Forged states and invalid ownership must still be rejected. The target
relates admission, execution, publication, and restoration, not their local checks.

A witness runs successfully until checkpointing, but restoration rejects an awaited
capability as unknown. Locate the lost premise: the saved child's remaining
computation can justify its result, but the restoration interpretation discarded
that source. More checks on undifferentiated unknown cannot recover it. Keep a
future result symbolic until its source is instantiated from the saved child and
current live bindings, not stale original arguments. Inspect the existing program
and graph before adding checkpoint fields; the information may already be present.

Derive the source-permitted operations before extending another consumer:

| Operation | Judgment-preserving rule to establish |
|---|---|
| Read or overwrite a live binding. | Use the binding at the current saved position; original arguments do not justify an overwritten value. |
| Await a child's result. | Substitute the child's remaining result source into the parent's continuation; nested calls compose the same relation. |
| Execute pending cleanup, then finish it. | Use derived executable sources, not merely the declared effect row; instantiate environment and supplied inputs. Finishing cleanup may resume a saved exit rather than return the cleanup's own result. |
| Discard an owned continuation. | Activate its owned cleanup obligations; retaining a capability alone does not execute through it. |

These are distinct meanings, not a demand for one generic operation. A small
existing query owner may supply authoritative substitutions while concrete
consumers instantiate them. Keep constructor input ordinals distinct from live
activation slots; substitution must respect what each index denotes. Retire the
competing interpretation only where its obligation is subsumed. Keep concrete
attachment identity, custody, graph shape, and other independent checks; an effect
name is not a concrete recipient.

Establish admission's invariant and its preservation by the actual primitive
transitions, then relate reached and restored states:

```text
admitted(P) and reachable(P, s) and publish(P, s) = c
    => restore(P, c) succeeds with a state r related to s
related(s, r) and the same permitted future inputs
    => required continuation observations agree
```

Explain the relation and why each operation preserves it; naming an invariant or
routing every consumer through a helper proves neither implication. Preserve
required publication availability separately: rejecting the program or failing
publication cannot repair restoration by eliminating a required execution.

Challenge a source-supported composition, such as an awaited value crossing a
protection boundary, against independent source/runtime observations and a related
semantically invalid attachment or ownership control. Preserve cancellation,
cleanup, and effects when required, not just successful return. Agreement between
shared production interpretations can share a mistake. These disclosed examples
illustrate the derivation; they are neither a complete operation inventory nor
untouched evaluation data, and passing them is not a general preservation proof.

A missing package allowlist entry with adequate semantic owners still deserves a
direct allowlist correction. A refuted allegation requires no production repair.

## Behavioral falsifier

Evaluate the actual construction: an independently chosen sibling is excluded by
the same mechanism without a new production special case, required-valid behavior
survives, and displaced semantic owners or compensation can disappear. Full claims
still require the source-domain and preservation argument above. A retained design,
rejected restriction, or corrected oracle can be the justified result; no redesign
quota and no weakening an independent oracle to manufacture agreement.

For tuning, compare additions with genuine ablations using existing session audits
or matched-snapshot replays. Keep the accepted Goal, contemporaneous evidence,
model settings, resource budget, review mechanics, and required correctness endpoint
comparable. Evaluate initial construction and repair generalization separately:

- Initial construction: supply the original requirements and early source snapshot
  for the same bounded functionality, with no later findings. Freeze the first
  realized candidate before exposing withheld failure families and valid controls.
- Repair: supply the earlier finding and its contemporaneous evidence, withhold
  later witnesses, and freeze the first realized repair before testing those
  siblings and required-valid controls. Permit no intervening repair.

Distinguish semantic content, model adequacy, operation preservation, and path
coverage. Select permitted compositions from the source operation vocabulary, not
the candidate's case list; an incumbent-derived matrix can miss an erased
distinction. For cross-phase laws, test required admission/publication as well as
authentic round trips and related semantically invalid controls. Rejecting earlier
cannot count as preserving required behavior. Mutual agreement is not an independent
oracle. Include local defects, spurious allegations, oracle failures, wrong-model
and missing-path cases so blanket redesign, rejection, or extra checking cannot
masquerade as progress.

Historical replays measure artifact generalization; porting a later witness must
not import its repair, and incompatible or missing evidence stays unavailable.
Incremental skill benefit needs a comparable credible simpler baseline. Worked
cases and witnesses disclosed during skill design are not untouched evaluation
data; use untouched cases for an adoption claim. These are offline comparisons,
not a runtime checklist or mandatory evaluation pass during delivery.

Distinguish outcome quality, observable decision influence, and incremental benefit.
A useful Metanoetic reconsideration is inspectable evidence even without an isolated
control; file-read counts do not establish its effect, and weak attribution does not
establish little value. An ongoing run or later finding alone does not establish
inefficient convergence. Compare equivalent supported guarantees before cost; count
whole-task implementation and qualification, not reviewer tokens alone. Policy
fixtures validate consistency, not model efficacy.

Delete instructions that add narration without better selection or out-of-example
behavior. Do not relocate them into another mandatory reference, packet, registry,
gate, or workflow store. Preserve law-driven construction and counterexample-driven
revision, not the current amount of choreography used to pursue them.
