# Independent reconstruction

Use when explicitly requested, or when Actuating judges that deriving a design
or explanation without the accumulated rationale could materially improve a
task-relevant decision. The user need not name the technique.

Reasons may include repeated repair under a disputed explanation, an unsupported
incumbent assumption constraining the candidate set, an unresolved consequential
representation or ownership choice, or a fresh-eyes request that would benefit
from a separate reasoning context. These are examples, not prerequisites. Do not
require proof of anchoring, an advance alternative, or a failed ordinary pass.

Select proportionately: a settled question, straightforward task, or uncertainty
resolved by a direct source lookup does not need another context. A long history
alone is not a reason. Reuse applicable reconstruction rather than adding routine
fan-out. Keep autonomous selection internal; return the resulting work, not a
selection receipt.

Existing delegation authority, privacy limits, and resource ceilings still govern.
Respect a no-delegation instruction. Choosing this technique does not authorize
tool installation, new paid evaluations, additional data disclosure, or increased
budgets; missing authority blocks only that effect, not independent authorized work.

This is a discretionary technique, not a public mode, CLI command, default
architecture pass, or review lane. A fresh-eyes request, `$glaze`, or `$metanoetic`
may motivate selection but does not mandate another worker. Their independent
entry points and canonical passages remain unchanged; reconstruction is never a
prerequisite for them or for Universalist.

The intervention separates requirements and evidence from the parent's persuasive
account of why the incumbent is necessary. It does not erase the existing code,
require a different answer, or claim independence from all prior knowledge.

## Start a genuinely separate context

Use an existing authorized new-context agent facility; inspect its actual context
inheritance and permission controls before selecting it. Start one read-only
reconstruction, not a standing subagent or new toolchain. Preserve the caller's
model and reasoning effort when supported; disclose a forced difference rather
than treating it as the same experimental condition. Stay within the task budget.

A full-history fork, a renamed worker, or a new worktree alone does not isolate
rationale. Do not pass the parent transcript, its design-defense summary, or a
saved answer as the starting context. Account for automatically inherited context
or memory carrying that rationale. If the available facility cannot exclude it,
report that rationale independence is unavailable or unestablished; do not simulate
isolation by asking the same context to forget. Continue independent authorized
work without provisioning tools or silently substituting an ordinary fork.

## Give evidence before the incumbent's argument

Reuse the source-bound Goal and current Construction Working Set. Supply a concise
brief directly in the agent request, not a new durable packet:

- accepted requirements, non-goals, source-fixed architecture, compatibility,
  authority, resource ceilings, and proof obligations;
- repository and exact base/head or other current subject, source locations, and
  the relevant code or read access to it;
- observed failures, known counterexamples, prior disconfirming results, relevant
  measurements, their provenance and applicability, and material uncertainty;
- the question to reconstruct and the read-only boundary.

Preserve factual reasons an earlier route failed, even when they favor the
incumbent. Initially withhold only the parent's persuasive interpretation, preferred
solution, and rejection narrative, not evidence or user-owned decisions. When fact
and rationale cannot be separated, preserve the evidence and disclose the exposure.
Source comments or design documents may reveal rationale; do not censor required
source or claim blindness if that occurs. Source access is not context isolation.

Ask the worker to derive an approach or explanation from that basis, naming its
assumptions, source support, unresolved questions, and a useful discriminator when
one emerges. A caller sketch, derivation, or proposed test can suffice. The worker
may recover the incumbent, offer a different approach, or remain uncertain. Do not
require an advance alternative, a fixed candidate count, or a full implementation.

The worker must not edit files or the index, create a worktree, run commands that
write generated output, capture memories, launch a review campaign, publish, or
claim completion. Use read-only tool controls where available; instructions alone
are not a sandbox. Missing enforcement is a limitation, not permission to expand
authority. Pass public/source evidence and conclusions, not private reasoning logs.

## Reveal, reconcile, and investigate

Retain the worker's initial conclusion in the existing conversation before
revealing the incumbent's rationale. Then provide that rationale and ask it to
reconcile both accounts against the same accepted requirements and evidence.
Distinguish changes caused by newly supplied facts from reconsideration of the
same facts. Do not retroactively describe the post-reveal answer as blinded.

Actuating evaluates any material disagreement through its existing architecture,
counterexample, and proof guidance. Prefer a decisive source argument, caller
example, or small discriminating test over a second complete implementation.
Any experiment belongs to Actuating under current mutation authority and outside
an open review epoch; the read-only reconstruction grants no experimental effects.
If the source or head changes, refresh applicability before selection.

Accept, adapt, reject, or retain through the existing selector. Agreement is not
independent proof; disagreement is not a defect or a reason to prefer novelty.
An unresolved difference blocks only dependent claims or actions under the existing
contract. Return the useful conclusion and material limitations, not an invocation
report or new receipt. A stronger explanation can be the result without an edit.

Stop the reconstruction when the question is answered sufficiently for the owning
decision, no useful discriminator remains, the task budget is reached, or missing
evidence/access prevents further necessary inquiry. Reuse applicable conclusions;
a new task-relevant question or materially changed evidence may warrant another
reconstruction, but a new label, reviewer, or commit alone does not. Do not spawn
fresh contexts repeatedly until one disagrees. Continue the enclosing authorized
task normally.
This technique earns no CAS/Elenctic review credit, changes no review counts or
reset rules, and neither replaces nor gates Glaze, Metanoetic, or Universalist.

## Validation scope

The technique is an unvalidated context intervention, not a claim of improved
intelligence. For a separately authorized comparison, keep task checkpoint,
requirements, evidence/tool access, model/effort, and budget comparable; vary the
initial rationale exposure deliberately. Test with and without the same doctrine
passage only when that factor is part of the experiment. Count failures and
limitations; score required behavior, correctness, useful discoveries, and cost,
not novelty or vocabulary. Existing Tune probes own change-validation examples;
no benchmark or extra review becomes a runtime prerequisite.
