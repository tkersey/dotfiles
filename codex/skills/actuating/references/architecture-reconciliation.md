# Architecture Reconciliation

Actuating reconstructs the incumbent from the exact current Git tree. Historical
analyses are hypotheses.

## Incumbent construction

Inspect:

```text
admitted representations and state dimensions
constructors, parsers, schemas, transitions, and capabilities
sanctioned producers and trusted consumers
owner set and authority
compatibility, migration, retry, recovery, and serialization paths
public and internal bypasses
primary enforcement and derived guards
required observations and proof mechanisms
```

## Compilation sequence

1. Preserve finding provenance and classify applicability and Goal-law authority.
2. Complete the initial falsification wave and close one cumulative evidence cut.
3. Build observational classes and causal generators.
4. Infer the invalid family and its predicted siblings or exhaustive domain.
5. Construct the complete admission graph.
6. Identify the earliest enforceable cut and canonical owner.
7. State the required admitted carrier and producer-factorization theorem.
8. Run one bounded `$metanoetic` challenger when the family, carrier, owner, cut,
   representation, or solution class may be wrong or incumbent-captive.
9. **OPERATE ARCHITECTONICALLY.**
10. Ask `$universalist` to lower every surviving direction into repository-native
    candidates.
11. Ask `$reduce` to identify duplicate owners and factors made redundant by each
    candidate.
12. Select by constructional dominance before realization cost.

Metanoetic remains before Universalist. It generates; Actuating adjudicates;
Universalist lowers; Reduce challenges residue.

## Construction theorem

A candidate must state:

```text
law and invalid family
current and successor admitted domains
canonical owner and earliest enforceable cut
successor carrier / constructor / representation / transition
every sanctioned producer and its migration
every bypass and its disposition
trusted consumers
required-valid and compatibility preservation
downstream compensators retired or demoted
family-level or exhaustive proof
construction falsifier
```

Architecture is closed only when the incumbent already satisfies this theorem.
A collection of guards that rejects observed examples is not a carrier-level
construction.

## Mutation routes

### Construction normalization

Required when any theorem element changes. The complete producer migration and
bypass closure are part of the architecture, not follow-up cleanup.

### Isolated restoration

Permitted only when the theorem already holds and the defect is localized to
realization or proof. The existing pure Ledger gate must admit the exact repair.

Unknown topology, ownership, family completeness, or path coverage is
`unresolved`, not permission for a local patch.

## Constructional dominance

`K2` dominates `K1` when both preserve required-valid behavior and `K2`:

```text
excludes at least the same invalid family
moves the law to an earlier or equal enforceable cut
factors more sanctioned producers through one canonical owner
has fewer independent primary semantic owners or compensators
provides a stronger family-level proof
```

Only constructionally equivalent candidates are compared by implementation size
or immediate cost.

## Working Set

Retain ephemerally:

```text
Goal / base / exact head
counterexample basis / family / siblings
admission graph / producers / consumers / bypasses
canonical owner / earliest cut / admitted carrier
producer migrations / compensator dispositions

```

No schema, registry, score, event log, or durable architecture store.
