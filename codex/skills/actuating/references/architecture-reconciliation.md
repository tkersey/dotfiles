# Architecture Reconciliation

Actuating reconstructs the incumbent from the exact current Git tree. Historical
analyses and model-authored path lists are hypotheses.

## Incumbent construction

Inspect:

```text
admitted representations and state dimensions
constructors, parsers, schemas, transitions, and capabilities
sanctioned producers and trusted consumers
transformations and composition edges
owner set and authority
compatibility, migration, retry, recovery, and serialization paths
public and internal bypasses
primary enforcement and derived guards
required observations and proof mechanisms
```

## Source-derived topology authority

Before architecture nomination, choose the strongest repository-native authority
for the live seam and derive predecessor topology `T0` from it. Suitable
authorities include closed type reflection, compiler IR or schema traversal,
public export inventories, route/build registries, actual graph reachability, and
generated producer inventories.

Record the derivation or generator, exact predecessor head, domain identity,
evidence strength, and falsifier. A handwritten topology or omission list may be
a regression witness; it cannot establish exhaustive coverage.

## Compilation sequence

1. Preserve finding provenance and classify applicability and Goal-law authority.
2. Complete the initial falsification wave and close one cumulative evidence cut.
3. Build observational classes and causal generators.
4. Infer the invalid family and its predicted siblings or exhaustive domain.
5. Derive `T0` independently from repository authority.
6. Identify the earliest enforceable cut pressure and carrier requirements.
7. Run one bounded `$metanoetic` challenger when the family, carrier, owner, cut,
   representation, or solution class may be wrong or incumbent-captive.
8. **OPERATE ARCHITECTONICALLY.**
9. Give one independently governed axis, one typed hole, and `T0` to
   `$universalist`. Split independently governed seams before recomposition.
10. Require Universalist to nominate a canonical cut `K`, total topology
    transformation `tau`, and total disposition law `F` over `T0`.
11. Select the repository-native exact-head verifier that will independently
    derive successor topology `T1` and prove transformation and factorization
    closure before mutation begins.
12. Ask `$reduce` to identify duplicate owners and factors made redundant by each
    candidate.
13. Select by constructional dominance before realization cost.

Metanoetic remains before Universalist. It generates; Actuating adjudicates;
Universalist nominates a total boundary transformation over the supplied domain;
Reduce challenges residue; Actuating proves and selects.

## Construction theorem

A complete candidate must state:

```text
law and invalid family
required-valid behavior and observations
source-derived predecessor topology T0 and its authority
canonical owner, earliest enforceable cut K, and admitted carrier
Universalist topology transformation tau
total disposition law F and explicit residuals
exact-head successor topology T1
T1 = tau(T0)
domain(F) = T1
every factorized producer-to-consumer route crosses K
every residual and bypass is explicitly owned
required-valid and compatibility preservation
downstream compensators retired or demoted
family-level and source-derived factorization proof
construction falsifier
```

For an open topology, a source-derived generator and preservation proof replaces
finite equality. Without either exhaustive derivation or an honest generator,
the strongest admissible result is `bounded` or `contained`, not complete
factorization or elimination.

Architecture is closed only when the incumbent already satisfies this theorem. A
collection of guards or an omission matrix that rejects observed examples is not
a carrier-level construction.

## Mutation routes

### Construction normalization

Required when any theorem element changes. The topology transformation, complete
producer migration, residual ownership, bypass closure, and exact-head
factorization proof are part of the architecture—not follow-up cleanup.

### Isolated restoration

Permitted only when the complete source-derived construction theorem already
holds and the defect is localized to realization or proof. The existing pure
Ledger gate must admit the exact repair.

Unknown topology authority, ownership, family completeness, transformation, or
path coverage is `unresolved`, not permission for a local patch.

## Constructional dominance

`K2` dominates `K1` when both preserve required-valid behavior and `K2`:

```text
excludes at least the same invalid family
uses an equally strong or stronger repository-derived topology basis
moves the law to an earlier or equal enforceable cut
provides a more total factorization over the exact-head topology
has fewer independent primary semantic owners or compensators
provides a stronger family-level and factorization-closure proof
```

Only constructionally equivalent candidates are compared by implementation size
or immediate cost.

## Revocation

A review finding that exposes a sanctioned producer, consumer, composition edge,
adapter, or bypass absent from the topology basis directly falsifies the topology
theorem. Revoke topology and factorization closure immediately; do not classify
it as realization-local repair or append it to the handwritten list.
