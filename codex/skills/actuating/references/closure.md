# Live Closure

Closure is a current judgment over owner facts, not stored workflow state.

Let:

```text
G  current accepted Goal
H  exact clean Git head/tree
V  exact validation bound to H
R  exact CAS review receipts bound to H and current review context
P  current Ship/provider publication evidence
F  current Review Fold and cumulative retained evidence cut
A  current architecture-reconciliation judgment
S  selected theory, causal basis, factor fold, and proof posture
```

```text
Close(G, H, V, R, P, F, A, S)
  -> continue | ready-to-ship | complete | blocked
```

## Finding authority

```text
entailed          current counterexample liability
strengthening     non-blocking follow-up unless adopted
preference        rejected as current liability
new-requirement   reopen Goal authority
underdetermined   blocked pending authority
```

Unadopted strengthening and preference do not expand the Goal.

## Cumulative evidence completeness

Closure requires the complete retained applicable corpus for the accepted Goal,
not only the latest campaign:

```text
all retained finding provenance and applicability
all class and causal-generator mappings
all executable finding witnesses
all required-valid behavior and observation proofs
all compatibility and migration proofs
all current elimination falsifiers and sibling probes
```

A changed head invalidates head-bound proof. It does not erase still-applicable
counterexample knowledge.

## Elimination lease

A current `entailed` witness that falsifies an eliminated family inside its
validity horizon revokes the lease, blocks closure, and requires failed-premise
localization before mutation. Read
[post-elimination-falsification.md](post-elimination-falsification.md).

## First or successor elimination

An `eliminated` disposition requires:

```text
sound interpretation and Goal adequacy
complete finding-to-class-to-generator or proved-exception mapping
family-level abstract exclusion and realization
complete sanctioned-path/frontier/cut coverage
current witness rejection
whole-corpus factor fold complete
passing quotient candidates selected
all replacements, collapses, retirements, privatizations, and bypass closures
  realized
finite-domain exhaustion or pre-implementation sibling predictions and probes
claim strength no stronger than weakest evidence
```

Repaired examples and direct-repair gate materialization are not family proof.

## Safety and normalization

Closure distinguishes:

```text
safe
  invalid family excluded at claimed strength

normal
  affected production and proof topology is derived from current causal
  generators and obligations rather than historical wounds
```

A safe but wound-shaped realization is not complete.

`normal` requires:

```text
every affected factor has exactly one evidence-backed disposition:
  preserve | replace | collapse | retire | privatize | distinct-obligation
no factor is dominated by a candidate that passed the same retained witnesses,
  required-valid proofs, and compatibility proofs
one family mechanism at the canonical owner or minimal cut per generator
no member-specific factor without separation proof
no duplicated semantic truth across owners unless independently required
proof recomposed at the strongest feasible generator level
subsumed example-specific proof machinery collapsed while provenance remains
```

The relevant complexity measure is independent semantic truths and enforcement
sites, not raw lines.

## Local implementation

`$actuating implement` is locally complete only when:

- Goal permits mutation and local outcome is met;
- exact current tree realizes the selected target and all factor dispositions;
- exact-head validation passes;
- every current finding has authority and applicability classification;
- every retained finding maps to a generator or proved exception;
- every retained witness remains rejected through sanctioned paths;
- every required-valid behavior and observation remains admitted;
- every compatibility obligation remains satisfied;
- every generator has one family-level mechanism;
- no dominated or unowned production or proof factor remains;
- no elimination lease is revoked without successor adjudication;
- every contained residual is authorized, owned, observable, and tested;
- no requested local operation or blocker remains.

## Final closeout

Final `complete` additionally requires:

- required Ship/provider state matches exact head;
- review context binds exact Goal, base, head, Review Contract, and optional
  pre-review Ship observation;
- all auxiliaries have terminal semantic verdicts;
- five consecutive distinct standard attempts are clean on unchanged head;
- every finding passed through Review Fold;
- no accepted applicable finding remains;
- no review finding silently widened Goal;
- no hotspot is answered only by compensating detection;
- no established generator is answered by wound-specific production or proof;
- no passing subtractive candidate was bypassed for additive repair;
- no post-elimination witness remains an unchanged-theory patch;
- no later evidence invalidates Goal, corpus, theory, factor fold, architecture,
  realization, validation, publication, or review.

A non-publicly complete implementation is `ready-to-ship`.

## Blockers

Return `blocked` for:

- missing source or repository authority;
- unknown finding authority or applicability;
- unadjudicated new requirement;
- unresolved theory, causal basis, factor topology, interpretation, owner,
  admission cut, or proof;
- incomplete retained corpus;
- an accepted finding without generator or separation proof;
- an affected factor without one complete disposition;
- a plausible subtractive candidate not executed before addition;
- a passing subtractive candidate bypassed by additive repair;
- direct repair attempted without failed subtractive comparison and valid gate;
- same-generator member-specific repair without separation proof;
- successor elimination based on repaired examples;
- absent sibling prediction or exhaustive proof;
- unsupported recurrence, dominance, normalization, or elimination claim;
- validation, realization, retirement, publication, or review failure;
- unauthorized containment;
- stale or unresolvable CAS evidence.

## Invalidation

```text
Goal changed
  -> recompile authority, corpus applicability, and theory

head changed
  -> refresh factor topology and rerun head-bound proof

new finding
  -> Review Fold, cumulative corpus update, causal and factor refold

subtractive candidate changed
  -> rerun identical negative and positive proof surface

post-elimination same-claim falsifier
  -> revoke lease and localize failed premise

direct-repair inputs changed
  -> prior pure materialization inapplicable

publication/review context changed
  -> prior bindings no longer match
```

Actuating reports the live verdict and materializes no closure receipt or durable
corpus, candidate, factor, or gate state.
