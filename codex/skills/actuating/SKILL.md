# Actuating

Turn accepted intent and accumulated falsification evidence into one lawful
Construction, its exact realization, independent review, and an evidence-backed
closure judgment.

## Authority kernel

Use exactly four authoritative per-goal artifact families:

1. `goal-contract/v3` — accepted semantics, authority, scope, compatibility,
   laws, and acceptance, compiled by `$goal-contract`.
2. `counterexample-set/v1` — classified witnessed falsifications, authored by
   `$review-fold`.
3. `construction-contract/v5` — the selected typed semantic model,
   proof-carrying predecessor-successor realization relation, concrete candidate
   comparison, proof, supersession, and retirements, authored by `$actuating`.
4. `actuating-evidence-event/v1` — append-only observations whose event bodies
   retain their domain owners.

The Goal Contract is the sole semantic authority. The Counterexample Set is the
sole classified-bug artifact. The Construction Contract is the sole
architecture-selection artifact. The Evidence Ledger is the sole mutable
per-goal truth. Read [artifact-kernel.md](references/artifact-kernel.md).

`$review-fold` classifies evidence but never chooses a correction.
`$first-principles` controls admissible premises. `$universalist` nominates
concrete semantic models and boundary artifacts. `$metanoetic` may expand the
hypothesis space once on a live decision surface. `$reduce` challenges,
quotients, and ablates factors. Actuating alone selects the Construction,
grants mutation, chooses the next action, evaluates review credit, and closes.
`$ship` alone owns public effects.

Ledger materializes, validates, appends, replays, and projects owner-authored
content. It never interprets review or Ship, selects a Construction, grants
mutation, or authors semantic closure.

Before the first Ledger command, load `$ledger`, complete `$ledger ensure`, and
require Ledger 1.x with `ledger-artifact-abi/v1`, Seq 1.x with
`seq-observation-abi/v1`, and successful checks for every selected passive
definition.

## Public routes

| Intent | Route | Mutation | Terminal result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Bare `$actuating` or `/goal $actuating` | implement -> Ship -> review-closeout | Authority-bound | `complete` |
| `$actuating implement` | implementation only | Authority-bound | Local `complete` |
| `$actuating triage` | acquire and classify review | Forbidden | Counterexample Set and report |
| `$actuating remediation-plan` | propose a successor Construction | Forbidden | Non-executable Construction Contract |
| `$actuating review-closeout` | normalize, repair, Ship when required, and re-review | Authority-bound | `complete` |

An unqualified request to review, inspect, audit, or classify selects `triage`.
Mutation requires explicit implement, fix, resolve, address, or closeout intent.

These are authority and workflow routes. They are not architectural change
classes. Do not call them modes.

## Construction selection

Before every architecture or abstraction selection:

1. Compile the current Goal and resolve the complete current Construction and
   Counterexample lineage.
2. Invoke `$first-principles` before the first Universalist nomination for every
   materially new candidate universe. Freeze the incumbent-independent basis
   before comparing the incumbent.
3. Build the cumulative Counterexample Theory from every currently applicable
   accepted stable class across the Goal lineage. Duplicate witnesses, campaign
   partitioning, reviewer identity, and finding order do not create new classes.
4. Quotient the theory into causal generators. Every accepted class belongs to
   exactly one current generator or one proof-carrying instance-specific
   separation.
5. Define a typed semantic element for every selected owner, state dimension,
   event, transition, effect, custody rule, observation, terminal condition,
   illegal state, and equivalence or normalization law.
6. Create exactly one predecessor carrier claim for every semantic element.
   Bind every claim to the exact predecessor subject and, when present, the
   predecessor Construction.
7. Ask `$universalist` for concrete repository-native candidates. Each candidate
   carries an actual boundary, architecture, semantic model, and factor set.
   Do not use transformation labels as substitutes for those objects.
8. Invoke `$metanoetic` exactly once when the current model, owner,
   representation, or candidate universe may be wrong. Universalist lowers every
   material result.
9. Invoke `$reduce` once when a candidate adds or preserves a disputable owner,
   representation, mechanism, bypass, compatibility path, recovery path, proof
   path, or residue.
10. Actuating selects the smallest non-dominated candidate satisfying the full
    obligation core. Reordering the same theory must yield an equivalent
    Construction or the same explicit incomparable minima.
11. Materialize and register `construction-contract/v5` with the exact
    predecessor verifier receipts required by its carrier claims.

Read [construction-contract.md](references/construction-contract.md).

## Mode-free realization correspondence

A Construction describes and proves the delta. It never names the delta.

The following fields are forbidden:

```text
mode
change_kind
repair_class
semantic_model_disposition
supersession_disposition
```

Each typed semantic element has exactly one carrier claim:

```text
closed-existing
  carrier exists on the exact predecessor subject
  owner-controlled construction surface is closed
  presence and closure receipts pass

open-existing
  carrier exists
  a bypass, competing owner, or competing representation is witnessed
  a concrete factor introduction, retirement, or replacement must close it

absent
  a bounded nonexistence witness proves no predecessor carrier exists
  a concrete factor introduction or replacement must create it

unresolved
  the available evidence establishes neither presence nor absence
  mutation is forbidden
```

A claim is not evidence merely because Actuating authored it. Every successor
claim that relies on a predecessor carrier cites content-addressed registration
receipt wrappers carrying the existing `actuating-verifier-receipt/v1` body,
bound to the current Goal, exact predecessor Construction and subject, claim,
obligation, verifier invocation, exit status, and output digests. An initial
Construction claims no predecessor preservation; it introduces the first total
relation and must prove the realized successor before closure.

The selected semantic model is total over realization:

```text
every semantic element
  -> exactly one carrier claim
  -> exactly one selected successor factor binding
  -> one owner-controlled constructor or representation
  -> bounded realization paths
  -> implementation and closure obligations
```

Every selected factor must carry at least one semantic element. Empty unmapped
production or proof surfaces remain structural requirements.

## Difference-triggered law

Do not infer obligations from a label. The actual relation triggers them:

```text
absent carrier
  -> introduced or replacement factor + implementation proof

open carrier
  -> introduced, retired, or replacement factor + closure proof

changed representation, owner, boundary, or admitted domain
  -> factor replacement or introduction + preservation proof

introduced factor
  -> governing laws + realization + proof

retired factor
  -> retirement record + absence verifier

unchanged factor
  -> exact predecessor-successor equality

removed semantic element
  -> retirement or successor mapping

added semantic element
  -> carrier claim + factor binding + proof
```

A complete source rewrite is admissible when the typed semantic model,
representation, ownership, boundary, admitted domain, factors, and observations
are genuinely preserved. Conversely, an unchanged abstract semantic model does
not conceal a representation or ownership replacement.

No derived reporting projection may grant authority. A set-valued diagnostic
such as `representation-replaced` or `factor-introduced` may be calculated from
the admitted relation, but it is not stored as an authoritative classifier.

## Recurrence law

On the second accepted class sharing a causal generator, another pointwise
correction is forbidden.

The generator's carrier claim must cite at least one concrete structural
resolution: an introduced factor, retired factor, or replacement relation. The
only alternative is a non-example separation proof that removes the class from
the generator and places it in the proof-carrying instance-specific partition.

A stronger test without a structural correction is not sufficient.

## Realization and effects

A Construction is the semantic decision unit. A bounded operation is only an
effect-isolation unit.

For each selected edit:

```text
clean parent subject
-> prepare one exact operation
-> one bounded executor creates one provisional diff
-> Actuating inspects the complete diff and changed paths
-> Actuating commits exactly that operation
-> require one-parent clean successor
-> record parent -> successor
-> verify and falsify on the successor
```

Actuating may execute directly or delegate. Executor identity is not part of
correctness; the admitted operation, complete diff, exact changed paths,
successor commit, and proof evidence are.

Before Ship or fresh review, establish current-subject realization:

```text
every typed semantic element -> one current factor and construction surface
every selected factor -> one current semantic element
every live proof mechanism -> one current proof obligation
every claimed closed owner -> no unchecked bypass
every retirement -> actual absence or successor mapping
```

Each result is carried by a canonical `actuating-verifier-receipt/v1` bound to
the current v5 Construction and exact successor subject. Predecessor receipts
authorize the selected relation; successor receipts prove that implementation
realized it. Neither substitutes for the other.

Do not begin closure-grade review against a half-realized Construction.

## Review convergence

The static Review Contract remains unchanged:

- bind standard plus the existing four auxiliaries before dispatch;
- launch the initial 1+4 wave concurrently through owner-lived CAS requests;
- never cancel a sibling because another request finds a defect;
- recover a verdictless terminal failure only for that exact request, once;
- count the initial wave's standard clean as attempt one;
- run four later standard attempts serially;
- require five consecutive distinct standard cleans on the exact unchanged
  published subject;
- reset all review credit after any material subject change.

Every finding passes through `$review-fold`. Accepted classes join the complete
Counterexample Theory before Actuating selects mutation. A noncanonical
closure-grade review binding is rejected before its findings can authorize
mutation.

## Publication and closure

Bare invocation and publication-bearing `review-closeout` hand a current `ready-to-ship` proof to `$ship`. Actuating
never substitutes its own public effect or publication receipt.

Apply [closure.md](references/closure.md) only to the current Goal,
`construction-contract/v5`, complete Counterexample Theory, exact clean subject,
predecessor and successor verifier evidence, Ship evidence when required, and
current review credit.

`construction-contract/v5` is a hard cut. v1-v4 artifacts and Evidence stores
remain historical evidence only. Use the v5 Evidence store and never reinterpret
older bytes.
