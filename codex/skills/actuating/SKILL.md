# Actuating

Turn accepted intent and accumulated falsification evidence into one lawful
Construction, its exact realization, independent review, and an evidence-backed
closure judgment.

## Authority kernel

Use exactly four authoritative per-goal artifact families:

1. `goal-contract/v3` — accepted semantics, authority, scope, compatibility,
   laws, and acceptance, compiled by `$goal-contract`.
2. `counterexample-set/v1` — classified witnessed falsifications, authored by
   `$review-fold`.
3. `construction-contract/v4` — the selected semantic model, cumulative
   Counterexample basis, concrete candidate comparison, normal form, proof,
   supersession, and retirements, authored by `$actuating`.
4. `actuating-evidence-event/v1` — append-only observations whose event bodies
   retain their domain owners.

The Goal Contract is the sole semantic authority. The Counterexample Set is the
sole classified-bug artifact. The Construction Contract is the sole
architecture-selection artifact. The Evidence Ledger is the sole mutable
per-goal truth. Read [artifact-kernel.md](references/artifact-kernel.md).

`$review-fold` classifies evidence but never chooses a repair. `$first-principles`
controls admissible premises. `$universalist` nominates concrete semantic models.
`$metanoetic` may expand the hypothesis space once on a live decision surface.
`$reduce` challenges, quotients, and ablates factors. Actuating alone selects the
Construction, grants mutation, chooses the next action, evaluates review credit,
and closes. `$ship` alone owns public effects.

Ledger materializes, validates, appends, replays, and projects owner-authored
content. It never interprets review or Ship, selects a Construction, grants
mutation, or authors semantic closure.

Before the first Ledger command, load `$ledger`, complete `$ledger ensure`, and
require Ledger 1.x with `ledger-artifact-abi/v1`, Seq 1.x with
`seq-observation-abi/v1`, and successful checks for every selected passive
definition.

Read [evidence-ledger.md](references/evidence-ledger.md) for the exact committed
subject, Goal carry-forward, operation, capability, attachment, and projection
laws. v1-v3 stores are historical only for v4 selection; follow its hard-cutover
rule rather than reinterpreting old Construction bytes.

## Public modes

| Intent | Route | Mutation | Terminal result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Bare `$actuating` or `/goal $actuating` | implement -> Ship -> review-closeout | Authority-bound | `complete` |
| `$actuating implement` | implementation only | Authority-bound | Local `complete` |
| `$actuating triage` | acquire and classify review | Forbidden | Counterexample Set and report |
| `$actuating remediation-plan` | propose a successor Construction | Forbidden | Non-executable Construction Contract |
| `$actuating review-closeout` | normalize, repair, Ship when required, and re-review | Authority-bound | `complete` |

An unqualified request to review, inspect, audit, or classify selects `triage`.
Mutation requires explicit implement, fix, resolve, address, or closeout intent.

## Construction selection

At the beginning of every architecture or abstraction selection, state exactly:

```text
OPERATE ARCHITECTONICALLY
```

Then:

1. Compile the current Goal and resolve the complete current Construction and
   Counterexample lineage.
2. Invoke `$first-principles` before the first Universalist nomination for every
   materially new candidate universe. Freeze the incumbent-independent
   derivation before comparing the incumbent.
3. Build the **Counterexample Theory** from every currently applicable accepted
   stable class across the Goal lineage. A new subject changes freshness, not
   semantic history. Duplicate witnesses and review ordering do not create new
   classes.
4. Quotient the theory into a **causal basis**. Each accepted class maps to one
   current causal generator or to an evidenced instance-specific exception.
5. Apply the semantic-novelty firewall below.
6. Ask `$universalist` for concrete repository-native semantic models. Candidate
   models may compose admitted-domain restriction, representation change,
   ownership change, realization rewrite, ablation, and proof recomposition;
   these are transformation axes, not mutually exclusive candidate families.
7. Invoke `$metanoetic` exactly once when an initial commitment is high-regret,
   an accepted class exposes a model/owner/representation error, a causal
   generator recurs, or the incumbent remains a merely adequate local optimum
   and a materially different model is plausible. Universalist lowers every
   material result.
8. Invoke `$reduce` once when a candidate adds or preserves a disputable owner,
   representation, mechanism, bypass, compatibility path, recovery path, proof
   path, or residue. `dominated` requires the smaller admissible candidate.
9. Actuating selects the smallest non-dominated Construction satisfying the
   complete mandatory obligation core. Reordering the same complete
   Counterexample Theory must yield an equivalent selected Construction or an
   explicit set of incomparable minima.

Read [construction-contract.md](references/construction-contract.md) for the
full `construction-contract/v4` law.

## Semantic-novelty firewall

A finding may retain the current semantic model only when both conditions hold:

1. the exact required behavior is already explicit in the current
   Construction; and
2. the correction introduces or changes no semantic constructor.

Semantic constructors include:

```text
state dimension or value
event or transition
transition guard or ordering law
authority or custody transfer
generation, freshness, or correlation rule
timeout, cancellation, retry, or recovery mode
completion or shutdown mode
effect or observation path
compatibility mode
independent validator
proof family
```

If a correction requires any constructor absent from the current model, the
Construction—not merely its realization—is falsified. Select an
`architecture-repair` successor before mutation. `realization-repair` and
`ablation-repair` require an exact unchanged semantic model.

On the second accepted class sharing an evidenced causal generator, another
local realization repair is forbidden unless non-example separation proof
establishes that the cases are genuinely independent. Otherwise select an
architecture or ablation successor or block.

## Counterexample-guided normalization

Review evidence never maps directly to edits.

```text
new findings
-> Review Fold classification and quotienting
-> cumulative Counterexample Theory
-> causal basis
-> one Construction selection
-> complete realization and normalization
-> fresh exhaustive review
```

Knowledge is monotone; implementation is not. A successor may preserve, rewrite,
consolidate, or delete the incumbent. Historical implementation momentum grants
no authority.

The first review-entry subject remains immutable for forensic audit. A later
anchor may not masquerade as the beginning of the closeout path. Architecture
selection, however, is rooted in the complete current obligation set rather than
in a latest-delta patch history.

Read [review-accretion.md](references/review-accretion.md), whose retained path
now specifies Counterexample-Guided Normalization and supersedes the old
retrospective Review Accretion Gate.

## Realization exactness

A Construction is the semantic decision unit. A one-seam operation is only an
effect-isolation unit.

For each selected edit:

```text
clean parent subject
-> prepare one exact operation
-> one-seam-operator creates one provisional diff
-> Actuating inspects the complete diff and changed paths
-> Actuating commits exactly that operation
-> require one-parent clean successor
-> record parent -> successor
-> verify and falsify on the successor
```

Multiple seams may realize one Construction. Do not begin closure-grade review
against a half-realized successor.

Before Ship or fresh review, perform obligation-rooted semantic garbage
collection:

```text
Goal laws + cumulative causal basis + observations + compatibility + resources
-> selected semantic model
-> Construction factors
-> production bindings and proof bindings
```

Every live correctness-bearing production mechanism maps to one current factor.
Every live proof mechanism maps to one current proof obligation. Every
superseded mechanism is deleted, mapped to a successor owner, or retained by a
distinct current obligation. Unmapped surface blocks.

Proof follows causal generators, not wounds. When multiple classes share one
generator, recompose examples into the strongest feasible law-, property-,
model-, representation-, or transition-level proof. Retain only genuinely
distinct boundary witnesses and prove retirement of dominated examples.

## Review convergence

The static Review Contract remains unchanged in topology and strength:

- bind standard plus the existing four auxiliaries before dispatch;
- launch the initial 1+4 wave concurrently through owner-lived CAS requests;
- never cancel a sibling because another request finds a defect;
- recover a verdictless terminal failure only for that exact request, once;
- count the initial wave's standard clean as attempt one;
- run four later standard attempts serially;
- require five consecutive distinct standard cleans on the exact unchanged
  published subject;
- reset all review credit after any material subject change.

Repeated review is a stochastic falsification capability, not ceremony. Later
clean-streak attempts may discover material defects and remain mandatory.

Every finding passes through `$review-fold`. Accepted classes join the complete
Counterexample Theory before Actuating selects any mutation. A noncanonical
closure-grade review binding is rejected before its findings can authorize
mutation; a real defect may re-enter only through an independently lawful
falsifier.

Before closure-grade review, require the current CAS review preflight and
capability proof:

```bash
cas app-server preflight --cwd <repo> --profile review \
  --app-server-transport managed-ws --json
```

```text
cas_structured_review_v1=true
cas_workflow_bound_owner_lived_review_v1=true
```

Follow [review-contract.md](references/review-contract.md) for exact request,
receipt, publication, retry, and credit semantics.

## Publication and closure

Bare mode and publication-bearing review-closeout hand a current
`ready-to-ship` proof to `$ship`. Actuating never substitutes its own public
effect or publication receipt.

Apply [closure.md](references/closure.md) only to the current Goal,
`construction-contract/v4`, cumulative Counterexample Theory, exact clean
subject, owner evidence, Ship evidence when required, and current review credit.
Actuating authors `actuating-closure-receipt/v1`; Ledger validates its structure
but never decides its verdict.

## Fail closed

Block on:

- stale or missing Goal, Construction, Counterexample lineage, or clean subject;
- a materially new candidate universe without a current Axiomatic Construction
  Basis and Universalist lowering;
- an accepted class omitted from the cumulative Counterexample Theory;
- an accepted class without a causal generator or evidenced instance-specific
  exception;
- a local repair that introduces a semantic constructor absent from the current
  model;
- candidate comparison that depends on finding arrival order or omits a
  mandatory obligation;
- a selected Construction with unmapped production/proof surface or incomplete
  retirements;
- closure-grade review of a partially realized Construction;
- a noncanonical review binding contributing mutation-authorizing evidence;
- a public effect outside Ship;
- any supporting skill, Ledger, reviewer, or executor taking Actuating's
  semantic authority.

For `final-closeout` `complete`, also block on stale publication identity,
review tuple mismatch, unresolved request-local recovery, unresolved accepted
Counterexamples, or fewer than five current-subject standard cleans.
