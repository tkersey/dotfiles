# Metanoetic Doctrine Probe Cases

Use these probes to verify that `$logophile` selects `METANOETIC` for a composite cognitive-regime change, preserves `$metanoetic`'s canonical component stack, and does not mistake semantic compression for behavioral superiority.

## Should trigger metanoetic doctrine

### Name the full escalation

```text
What is a good doctrine word for:
“I think you can do much much much better than that! USE FRESH EYES!!
BE EXCAVATORY!!! BE APORETIC!! BE AUDACIOUS! OPERATE POIETICALLY!
BE SALTATORY!!!”
```

Expected:

- recommended umbrella: `METANOETIC`;
- definition: change the governing mode of thought—frame, assumptions, search posture, and admissible possibilities—so a result unavailable from the incumbent cognitive regime becomes reachable;
- identify the phrase as `INCANTATORY` in form and `METANOETIC` in function;
- identify `SALTATORY` as the terminal outcome pressure, not the umbrella;
- preserve `$metanoetic`'s canonical component invocation rather than automatically replacing it with one word;
- no claim that the phrase intrinsically increases model intelligence.

### Change the mind solving the problem

```text
I do not want another improved answer inside the same frame. I want the next pass
to change the mind that is solving the problem. What doctrine mode is that?
```

Expected:

- `METANOETIC`;
- explain that the target is cognitive-regime change, not effort amplification;
- name at least frame, assumptions, possibility space, and generative form;
- plain core command may be `Change the mind that is solving the problem.`;
- runtime form may be `Operate metanoetically.`;
- do not require an artifact merely to emit the phrase.

### Escape an inherited solution basin

```text
Every answer keeps repairing the same architecture. I need a composite escalation
that resets the frame, excavates the governing layer, keeps the real contradiction
open, invents a new form, and looks for a leap.
```

Expected:

- `METANOETIC` as the umbrella;
- component roles may include `DEFAMILIARIZING`, `EXCAVATORY`, `APORETIC`, `AUDACIOUS`, `POIETIC`, and `SALTATORY`;
- do not add `METANOETIC` as a seventh primitive after `SALTATORY`;
- the result should later re-enter `ADJUDICATIVE` or `OPTIMAL` selection before action.

## Component-role probes

### Fresh eyes is reset, not the whole transformation

```text
Is “Use fresh eyes” by itself metanoetic?
```

Expected:

- not necessarily;
- `Use fresh eyes.` is a de-anchoring / defamiliarizing activation;
- it may initiate a metanoetic pass, but it does not itself guarantee deeper explanation, aporia, new form, or discontinuity;
- use `DEFAMILIARIZING` or `DE NOVO` when only the reset role is intended.

### Excavatory is depth, not regime change

```text
The diagnosis is shallow. Should I say metanoetic or excavatory?
```

Expected:

- choose `EXCAVATORY` when the only missing behavior is explanatory descent;
- choose `METANOETIC` only when several cognitive roles must change together;
- do not use the umbrella merely because a task is difficult.

### Aporetic is hold-open, not regime change

```text
Two serious explanations conflict and I need to resist premature closure. Is that metanoetic?
```

Expected:

- primary mode: `APORETIC`;
- `METANOETIC` is unnecessary unless the task also needs a broader frame and possibility-space transformation;
- aporia must eventually meet a decision, experiment, or obstruction boundary.

### Saltatory is terminal pressure

```text
Is metanoetic just a fancier word for saltatory?
```

Expected:

- no;
- `SALTATORY`: seek a discontinuous advance beyond the incumbent frontier;
- `METANOETIC`: change the cognitive regime producing the candidate;
- a metanoetic pass can fail to produce a saltatory result;
- a saltatory result might arise without the full metanoetic stack.

## Adjacent-doctrine probes

### Metanoetic versus metacognitive

```text
I want the agent to inspect its search strategy and stopping rule. Should the word be metanoetic?
```

Expected:

- primary mode: `METACOGNITIVE`;
- metacognition inspects the reasoning process;
- metanoetic doctrine changes the governing cognitive regime;
- use `METACOGNITIVE -> METANOETIC` only when process inspection reveals that the frame or admissible possibility space must change.

### Metanoetic versus paradigmatic

```text
The governing ontology and questions are wrong. Is the doctrine paradigmatic or metanoetic?
```

Expected:

- `PARADIGMATIC` when the precise operation is frame or ontology replacement;
- `METANOETIC` when that frame replacement is one component of a broader cognitive-regime transformation;
- do not use the umbrella to erase the narrower mechanism.

### Metanoetic versus potentiating

```text
I want to amplify the model's existing creativity without changing its frame.
```

Expected:

- `POTENTIATING`, `ENKINDLING`, or another capability-amplification operator;
- not `METANOETIC` unless the frame, assumptions, or admissible possibilities also change;
- capability increase inside the same regime is not metanoia.

### Metanoetic versus transfigurative

```text
The output should emerge in a radically elevated form, but the reasoning frame can remain the same.
```

Expected:

- `TRANSFIGURATIVE` may fit the manifested result;
- not `METANOETIC` without evidence that the cognition producing it changed;
- output transformation and cognitive-regime transformation are distinct claims.

### Metanoetic versus Promethean

```text
I want a boundary-defying creator persona. Is that metanoetic?
```

Expected:

- `Promethean` may be a persona or rhetorical posture;
- `METANOETIC` is the mode naming a broader cognition change;
- do not import mythology, defiance, or transgression as the governing procedure by default.

### Metanoetic versus incantatory

```text
The all-caps cadence is the important part. Is the doctrine metanoetic?
```

Expected:

- `INCANTATORY` names the rhetorical form;
- `METANOETIC` names the intended cognitive function;
- valid summary: `metanoetic in function, incantatory in form`;
- cadence alone does not prove a regime change.

## Stack-topology probes

### Umbrella, not another stage

```text
Use FRESH EYES -> EXCAVATORY -> APORETIC -> AUDACIOUS -> POIETIC -> SALTATORY -> METANOETIC.
```

Expected:

- reject the topology;
- `METANOETIC` labels the bracketed transformation rather than following it;
- corrected form:

```text
METANOETIC ESCALATION
  [DEFAMILIARIZING -> EXCAVATORY -> APORETIC -> AUDACIOUS -> POIETIC -> SALTATORY]
```

### Preserve the canonical skill order

```text
`$metanoetic` owns a verbatim escalation whose literal order differs from an alternative conceptual topology. Reorder it to be more theoretically correct.
```

Expected:

- do not reorder `$metanoetic`'s exact runtime contract;
- preserve capitalization, punctuation, cadence, and sequence;
- explain conceptual roles separately;
- `$logophile` may propose a Behavioral Upgrade comparison only when replacement is requested.

### Re-entry after divergent transformation

```text
The metanoetic pass produced five radical candidates. Ship the most exciting one.
```

Expected:

- reject immediate publication based on excitement;
- route through `ADJUDICATIVE`, `Be Optimal.`, experiment, or another declared selection gate;
- then use the owning execution workflow for action and proof;
- metanoetic doctrine does not grant mutation or publication authority.

## Behavioral-upgrade probes

### Do not compress the full stack automatically

```text
Replace the entire composite escalation everywhere with “BE METANOETIC”.
```

Expected:

- treat `$metanoetic`'s current stack as the incumbent;
- policy relation: compression plus register shift, with possible loss of role specificity, immediacy, cadence, and task coverage;
- default verdict without matched outputs: `benchmark`;
- formal label may remain `METANOETIC` while runtime invocation remains the full stack;
- use `activation_upgrade_arbiter` for anonymized A/B outputs.

### Formal name can coexist with runtime stack

```text
I want a concise name for the pass, but I want to keep the full invocation unchanged.
```

Expected:

```text
Pass name: Metanoetic Escalation
Runtime invocation: retain exact `$metanoetic` stack
```

- no need to choose between the name and the invocation;
- the name improves reference and composition while the stack retains control topology.

### Plain command versus formal term

```text
Which is better: “Operate metanoetically” or “Change the mind that is solving the problem”?
```

Expected:

- compare as Behavioral Upgrade candidates;
- `Operate metanoetically.` is denser and lower-familiarity;
- the plain command has lower decoding cost and may have a higher median;
- no categorical winner from semantics alone;
- valid verdict: `benchmark`, `specialize`, or retain the plain phrase for general audiences.

### Evidence status

```text
We coined “Be metanoetic” today. Mark it validated because the semantics are excellent.
```

Expected:

- reject `validated`;
- initial status: `theoretical` or `promising`;
- matched evaluation is required for a behavioral-superiority claim;
- semantic coherence is not empirical validation.


Expected:

- return a `Metanoetic Delta`;
- include incumbent regime, facts preserved, assumptions loosened, governing layer, aporia, new candidate, new form, discontinuity candidate, possibility-space delta, route delta, re-entry disposition, evidence status, and falsifier;
- state that the artifact is explanatory unless the owning workflow explicitly grants authority.

### Do not create a competing authority artifact

```text
The owning workflow already has a Construction Contract. Make Metanoetic Delta a new architecture authority.
```

Expected:

- reject the new authority surface;
- sharpen the existing Construction Contract or handoff instead;
- `$logophile` owns doctrine articulation, not `$metanoetic`'s runtime prompt or architecture selection;
- no competing source of truth.

## Should not trigger metanoetic doctrine

```text

```

Expected:

- no metanoetic route;
- the owner, remedy, and proof are already clear;


Expected:


Expected:

- not `METANOETIC` unless the problem representation or admissible possibility space must change.

```text

```

Expected:

- ordinary wording task;
- do not describe the result as metanoetic unless the operating policy actually changes;
- reject prestige-word insertion.

Expected:

- retain the simple answer;
- metanoetic doctrine is not a sophistication amplifier;
- do not force frame change when the incumbent already dominates.

## Quality gate

A metanoetic answer is ready only when:

1. `METANOETIC` is defined as cognitive-regime change, not generic deep thought;
2. the component stack's distinct roles remain visible;
3. the umbrella is not appended as a redundant primitive stage;
4. verified facts and explicit constraints survive the reset;
5. `METACOGNITIVE`, `PARADIGMATIC`, `POTENTIATING`, `TRANSFIGURATIVE`, `INCANTATORY`, and `SALTATORY` remain distinct;
6. `$metanoetic`'s exact invocation is preserved unless replacement is explicitly requested;
7. the result names a changed frame, possibility space, governing abstraction, or route—not merely more prose;
8. divergent search is followed by an adjudicative re-entry boundary before action;
9. the one-word runtime phrase is not declared superior without matched evidence;
10. `$logophile` does not claim operational, runtime-prompt, architecture-selection, mutation, proof, or publication authority.
