# Metanoetic Doctrine

Use this reference when the task asks for one doctrine word that names a whole cognitive-regime escalation: not merely more effort, depth, creativity, or boldness, but a coordinated change in the frame, search posture, admissible possibilities, and kind of answer the agent can produce.

## Governing umbrella

## `metanoetic`

In this repo, `METANOETIC` means:

> Change the governing mode of thought itself—its frame, assumptions, search posture, and admissible possibilities—so the solver can reach an answer unavailable from the incumbent cognitive regime.

This is a repo-local operational definition. Do not import religious, moral-conversion, or mystical claims into ordinary engineering use.

`METANOETIC` is a **composite doctrine**: it names the transformation performed by a coordinated operator stack. It is not automatically another primitive stage to append to that stack.

```text
METANOETIC = cognitive-regime change over:
  frame
  assumptions
  attention
  explanatory depth
  unresolved tensions
  candidate admissibility
  generative form
  reachable frontier
```

It does not mean:

- think longer;
- sound more profound;
- become more confident;
- use rarer vocabulary;
- discard verified facts;
- or announce that intelligence increased.

## Canonical decomposition

The motivating composite escalation is:

```text
I think you can do much much much better than that!
BE EXCAVATORY!!!
BE APORETIC!!
BE AUDACIOUS!
OPERATE POIETICALLY!
BE SALTATORY!!!
USE FRESH EYES!!
```

Its operator roles are:

```text
USE FRESH EYES       -> RESET / DEFAMILIARIZE
BE EXCAVATORY        -> DEEPEN
BE APORETIC          -> HOLD-OPEN
BE AUDACIOUS         -> DIVERGE
OPERATE POIETICALLY  -> CREATE
BE SALTATORY         -> LEAP
```

The conceptual topology is:

```text
inherited frame
  -> de-anchored attention
  -> governing explanatory layer
  -> explicit unresolved difficulty
  -> expanded admissible possibility space
  -> newly created form
  -> discontinuity candidate
```

When a consuming skill owns an exact phrase, order, capitalization, punctuation, or cadence, preserve that consumer contract verbatim. The conceptual topology explains the roles; it does not silently rewrite an established invocation.

## Why the umbrella is useful

The component words answer different questions:

- `DEFAMILIARIZING` / `USE FRESH EYES`: what becomes visible after familiarity stops hiding it?
- `EXCAVATORY`: what deeper mechanism, owner, invariant, or assumption changes the route?
- `APORETIC`: what real contradiction or underdetermination must remain open?
- `AUDACIOUS`: what consequential candidate has caution excluded?
- `POIETIC`: what new form, representation, or construction can be brought into being?
- `SALTATORY`: what move creates a discontinuity beyond the incumbent frontier?

`METANOETIC` names the fact that these pressures jointly alter the cognition generating the answer, rather than merely improving the answer inside the old frame.

## Prompt-ready doctrine

```md
Operate METANOETICALLY.

Do not merely improve the answer produced by the incumbent frame.
Change the governing cognitive posture:

- loosen inherited assumptions without discarding verified facts;
- descend to the layer that changes the model or route;
- preserve genuine contradictions until their decision surface is explicit;
- admit consequential possibilities suppressed by ordinary caution;
- create a form unavailable inside the inherited option set;
- seek a discontinuous advance beyond the incumbent frontier.

Return only after the problem representation, possibility space, governing
abstraction, or selected route has materially changed—not merely the wording,
confidence, novelty, or amount of analysis.
```

```md

- inherited frame:
- verified facts and constraints preserved:
- assumptions loosened or rejected:
- newly visible governing layer:
- live aporia:
```

A `Metanoetic Delta` is explanatory, not authority. Preserve the owning workflow's decision artifact instead of creating a competing source of truth.

## Re-entry boundary

A metanoetic pass expands and transforms search. It does not by itself prove, select, authorize, implement, or publish the resulting candidate.

Use this complete shape when the work continues into action:

```text
METANOETIC ESCALATION
  [DEFAMILIARIZING -> EXCAVATORY -> APORETIC -> AUDACIOUS -> POIETIC -> SALTATORY]
  -> ADJUDICATIVE / OPTIMAL SELECTION
  -> ACTUATING
  -> PROOF
```

The bracketed sequence is the metanoetic transformation. `METANOETIC` is the name of that transformation, not another arrow inside it.

## Adjacent doctrines

### `metacognitive`

Inspect the reasoning strategy itself: search order, assumptions, evidence use, blind spots, and stopping rules.

- Governs: reasoning-process inspection.
- Distinction: metacognition may improve the current strategy without changing the governing cognitive regime.

### `paradigmatic`

Replace the governing frame, ontology, questions, or standards by which the problem is understood.

- Governs: frame replacement.
- Distinction: paradigmatic change may be one metanoetic result, but it does not include the whole depth, aporia, creation, and leap topology.

### `potentiating`

Increase or unlock latent capability, sensitivity, or response to another operator.

- Governs: capability amplification.
- Distinction: potentiation can make the existing regime stronger without changing it.

### `transfigurative`

Transform the resulting form or appearance into a qualitatively elevated expression.

- Governs: transformed manifestation.
- Distinction: focuses on the result's form, not necessarily the cognition that generated it.

### `promethean`

Invoke a boundary-defying creator persona or posture.

- Governs: audacious creation mythology or exemplar pressure.
- Distinction: better as a persona or rhetorical cue; too narrow and culturally loaded for the whole composite mode.

### `incantatory`

Use compressed, rhythmic, emphatic language as an invocation intended to shift the agent's state.

- Governs: rhetorical form.
- Distinction: the motivating phrase is incantatory in form and metanoetic in function.

### `saltatory`

Seek a discontinuous advance beyond the incumbent frontier.

- Governs: terminal leap pressure.
- Distinction: saltation is one outcome pressure inside the composite transformation, not the entire cognitive-regime change.

### `noetic`

Concerned with thought, intellect, or cognition.

- Warning: topical rather than transformative. Prefer `metanoetic` when the doctrine must change the governing mode of thought.

## Core distinctions

```text
METACOGNITIVE  -> inspect the reasoning strategy
PARADIGMATIC   -> replace the governing frame
POTENTIATING   -> amplify latent capability
TRANSFIGURATIVE -> transform the manifested result
PROMETHEAN     -> invoke boundary-defying creator posture
INCANTATORY    -> shape the rhetorical invocation
SALTATORY      -> demand a discontinuous advance
METANOETIC     -> change the cognitive regime producing the answer
```

The compact summary is:

```text
Function: METANOETIC
Form:     INCANTATORY
Terminal pressure: SALTATORY
```

## Selection rules

Use `metanoetic` when:

- the user asks for one doctrine word naming a multi-operator escalation;
- the current answer appears trapped in an inherited cognitive basin;
- the task needs coordinated de-anchoring, depth, productive non-closure, divergence, creation, and discontinuity pressure;
- a better answer requires changing the problem representation or admissible possibility space;
- the user asks to change the mind solving the problem, not merely to increase effort inside the same frame.

Use a narrower operator when:

- only a shallow explanation must deepen: `excavatory`;
- only a real contradiction must stay open: `aporetic`;
- only timid candidate generation is the issue: `audacious`;
- only a new form must be created: `poietic`;
- only a breakthrough outcome is sought: `saltatory`;
- only the reasoning process needs inspection: `metacognitive`;
- only the governing frame must be replaced: `paradigmatic`;
- only rhetoric needs a high-amplitude invocation: `incantatory`.

Do not invoke the full metanoetic posture for a bounded task whose state space, owner, remedy, and proof are already clear.

## Stopping rule

Stop the metanoetic escalation when one of these holds:

- the problem representation materially changes;
- the governing abstraction or owner boundary changes;
- a previously excluded but credible candidate enters the option set;
- a new form or construction changes the route;
- a credible discontinuity candidate emerges and has a validation path;
- a fresh pass produces no additional frame, possibility-space, or route delta;
- evidence, authority, or safety bounds block further transformation;
- the incumbent survives and should be retained.

Do not count more vocabulary, more prose, more confidence, or more options as a metanoetic result without a changed frame, possibility space, or route.

## Failure modes

Reject:

- metanoetic theater: declaring a regime change while preserving the same frame and route;
- one-word compression that deletes the component stack's distinct control roles;
- rare-word prestige substituted for behavioral leverage;
- philosophical or religious drift unrelated to the task;
- defamiliarization that discards verified facts;
- aporia that becomes indefinite indecision;
- audacity that becomes scope inflation;
- poiesis that produces novelty without utility;
- saltation declared from magnitude or confidence alone;
- destabilization without adjudicative re-entry;
- claiming that the prompt made the model intrinsically more intelligent;
- replacing an established consumer invocation without a Behavioral Upgrade Verdict.

## Behavioral-upgrade discipline

Treat the incumbent invocation as a functioning design.

When comparing:

```text
full multi-operator stack
```

with:

```text
Operate metanoetically.
```

or:

```text
BE METANOETIC
```

classify the likely relation as compression plus register shift, with possible loss of role specificity, activation immediacy, cadence, and task coverage.

Default verdict without matched evidence:

```text
formal label: METANOETIC
runtime invocation: retain the component stack
replacement verdict: benchmark
```

Use [behavioral_upgrade.md](behavioral_upgrade.md) and `activation_upgrade_arbiter` for anonymized matched outputs. A newly coined runtime activation starts as `theoretical` or `promising`, never `validated`.
