# Composition Guidance

Use `$logophile` as a compiler for decision-bearing language, not as an ambient
postprocessor after another workflow.

## Activation law

Do not invoke merely because a workflow produced prose.

Invoke only when changing the wording itself, while holding the underlying facts
constant, could materially change:

- behavior or the next action;
- interface use;
- authority or ownership;
- obligation, modality, or scope;
- recovery behavior;
- a durable public interpretation.

Routine summaries, proof lists, handoffs, status updates, and final answers remain
owned by the producing workflow. When the gate does not pass, do not add a
Logophile pass.

## Review adjudication

Eligible decision-bearing surfaces include:

- public dispositions or stable resolve-state labels;
- reviewer replies, rebuttals, and acknowledgements whose wording changes the
  accepted obligation or next action;
- criteria, materiality, evidence, confidence, and overturn conditions exposed
  to another actor;
- `validate-only`, `do-not-address`, `blocked`, or equivalent terms whose
  interpretation controls routing.

A routine finding summary is not an implicit trigger.

When eligible, preserve raw comment identity, disposition, evidence basis,
criteria, materiality, and what would change the ruling. Do not turn wording
work into adjudication or implementation.

## Fixed-point, closure, and Actuating outputs

Eligible surfaces include public or durable wording for:

- `ready`, `blocked`, `complete`, or reopen conditions;
- a command, mode, state, proof, or artifact name;
- an executable next-step instruction;
- a public guarantee or closure claim.

A routine list of checks, commits, tests, or current state is not an implicit
trigger.

When eligible, preserve gate status, proof receipts, unresolved blockers,
Actuating's lever, proof of movement, and next bottleneck. Do not soften an
executable step into a vague recommendation or strengthen a proof claim beyond
the evidence.

## Ablation and reduction

Eligible surfaces include stable names and public decisions for delete, keep,
collapse, canonicalize, decommission, or `not-required` dispositions.

When eligible:

- `ablation: not-required` must retain its evidence;
- keep/delete/collapse/canonicalize decisions must not sound like taste;
- the preservation or refinement relation must remain explicit;
- `isomorphic` must not replace `refinement-preserving` when behavior may
  intentionally contract.

Do not invoke merely because an implementation deleted code.

## Architecture and abstraction selection

Use [architectonic_doctrine.md](architectonic_doctrine.md) only when the language
decision names, contracts, or publicly explains a governing abstraction, owner
boundary, representation, factorization, composition law, or reconstitution
route.

When eligible, preserve:

- live obligations and required observations;
- incumbent abstractions and owners;
- abstraction leaks, accidental distinctions, and shadow truth;
- candidate organizing principles and governing factors;
- constructors, eliminators, and lawful compositions;
- canonical owners and unavoidable validation owners;
- conceptual compression;
- the reconstitution path;
- the preservation or refinement relation;
- proof, falsifier, and obstruction.

Keep these distinctions explicit:

- `ARCHITECTONIC` discovers the governing organization;
- `PRINCIPAL` finds the most general solution under constraints;
- `FACTORIZING` exposes factors and recomposition;
- `REPRESENTATION-SHIFTING` changes representation;
- `CANONICALIZING` selects one owner or normal form;
- `UNIVERSALIZING` requires a real factorization-and-uniqueness property;
- `MORPHOGENETIC` generates a new organizing form;
- `RECONSTITUTIVE` rebuilds around the selected form;
- `REIFYING` turns hidden behavior into explicit data.

When `$actuating` is the consumer, preserve exact
`OPERATE ARCHITECTONICALLY` compatibility and sharpen its existing decision
surfaces. `$logophile` does not nominate, select, implement, or verify the
architecture and must not create a competing authority artifact.

## Breakthrough and innovation language

Use [breakthrough_doctrine.md](breakthrough_doctrine.md) when wording claims or
requests a breakthrough, leap, step-change, regime transition, or newly reachable
capability.

Preserve:

- incumbent frontier;
- limiting assumption;
- discontinuity mechanism;
- newly reachable state;
- evidence that the result is a leap rather than an increment;
- remaining uncertainty and falsifier.

Do not polish novelty, magnitude, a large rewrite, or a metric jump into a
breakthrough claim. When evidence is incomplete, use `saltatory candidate`,
`speculative saltation`, or a weaker adjacent term.

For runtime-phrase replacement, use
[behavioral_upgrade.md](behavioral_upgrade.md); do not assume `Be saltatory.`
outperforms a plainer incumbent.

## Composite cognitive-regime escalation

Use [metanoetic_doctrine.md](metanoetic_doctrine.md) only when a live language
decision names or explains a coordinated escalation intended to change the
frame, assumptions, search posture, admissible possibilities, and kind of answer
the agent can produce.

Preserve:

- incumbent cognitive regime and inherited frame;
- verified facts and explicit constraints;
- distinct roles of defamiliarization, excavation, aporia, audacity, poiesis,
  and saltation;
- new governing layer, frame, or possibility space;
- newly admitted candidate or created form;
- route delta and discontinuity candidate;
- adjudicative re-entry boundary;
- evidence status and falsifier.

`METANOETIC` names the composite transformation; it is not another primitive
stage after `SALTATORY`. Preserve an owning skill's exact invocation rather than
collapsing it to `Be metanoetic.` without Behavioral Upgrade adjudication.

## Seq, Learnings, and precedent

Eligible surfaces include stable precedent names, governing rules, public
knowledge claims, and action-bearing summaries whose wording changes future
routing.

A routine source-backed summary is not an implicit trigger.

When eligible, preserve citations, source paths, timestamps, freshness, evidence
strength, analogy, distinguishing facts, supersession, confidence, and the
explicit action delta. Do not turn remembered context into authority by polishing
it.

## Simulation and world-modeling

Eligible surfaces include names and public contracts for:

- real-system and simulated boundaries;
- state, action, transition, and observation vocabulary;
- fidelity targets and unsupported zones;
- counterfactual interventions and held-constant assumptions.

Invoke only when the wording affects how the model may be used or trusted. Do not
polish an approximation into a prediction.

## Evaluation, grading, and judgment

Eligible surfaces include criteria, scale labels, dispositions, confidence
language, materiality, and overturn conditions that govern a decision.

When eligible, keep the operating mode (`ADJUDICATIVE`) separate from an
explicitly requested persona (`Arbiter`). Preserve evidence and counterevidence;
do not convert preference into defect language.

## Behavioral upgrades

Use [behavioral_upgrade.md](behavioral_upgrade.md) whenever proposed wording may
displace an incumbent that already steers behavior.

- Treat the baseline as the incumbent.
- Map baseline and candidate roles.
- Classify the policy delta.
- Compare familiarity, cadence, decoding cost, task coverage, median, ceiling,
  variance, and shadow risk.
- Return `retain`, `replace`, `specialize`, `pair`, `sequence`, or `benchmark`.
- Use `activation_upgrade_arbiter` for blinded matched-output adjudication when
  warranted.

Do not let semantic density, rare register, novelty, or generator preference
substitute for behavioral evidence.

## Naming modes, skills, and personas

Names are decision-bearing when they become stable commands, interfaces, states,
roles, or artifacts.

Prefer names that encode the job or success condition:

- `fixed-point-driver` names the convergence condition;
- `ablation_auditor` names the surface-removal audit;
- `actuating` names state-changing execution;
- `logophile` names the language craft layer.

Keep grammar aligned:

- mode: `NOMOTHETIC`; persona: `Nomothete`;
- mode: `ADJUDICATIVE`; persona: `Arbiter`;
- mode: `EMULATIVE`; persona only when a role noun adds value.

Avoid plumbing-only names such as `manager`, `coordinator`, `util`, `helper`, or
`processor`.

## Doctrine stacks

Doctrine or activation requests are explicit language decisions. Return:

1. recommended words or phrases;
2. distinct operator roles;
3. artifacts only when required by the receiving workflow;
4. optional persona and core command when requested;
5. final copy-pasteable wording.

When a stack replaces an incumbent, include a Behavioral Upgrade Verdict.

## Authority boundary

`$logophile` owns wording, terminology, semantic-preservation checks, and
human-facing articulation. It does not execute the operational workflow,
adjudicate its facts, select architecture, authorize mutation, verify evidence,
or publish.
