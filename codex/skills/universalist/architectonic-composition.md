# Architectonic composition

Inside `$architectonic`, Universalist is a read-only nominator for one evidenced
live semantic boundary decision, not a second architectural review. Architectonic
owns scope, assignment, adjudication, recommendation, and coverage. This binding
adds no trigger, route, proof profile, or standalone decision authority; all
applicable entry and decision-contract obligations remain.

Read this binding instead of the Actuating composition or standalone disposition
format. Reuse the supplied source-bound context and any equivalent challenger.
The assigned nomination owner supplies the incumbent, accepted requirements and
observations, current constraints, and semantic pressure. Missing premises remain
evidence debt; an architectural review is not authority to invent domain laws.
For a proposed design, treat attributed design evidence as proposed, not realized
repository enforcement. No live boundary pressure means no implicit invocation.

Return `candidate`, `preserve-incumbent`, `unresolved`, or `obstructed` with the
smallest argument Architectonic can adjudicate: owner and operation, law and
required-valid observations, material decision delta, representative boundary or
caller sketch, discriminator, and material transition/residual/invalidation costs.
Use Architectonic's existing assessment rather than a second report or receipt.
These supporting results do not create or rename Universalist routes.

Start with the incumbent and ordinary repository-native candidate. Carry forward
at most one grounded law-derived challenger, including a first-use boundary when
the actual law justifies it. Load recognition, consequential comparison, advanced
mechanics, and theory references only at their existing evidence gates. A review
with no mutations still needs consequential comparison before recommending a
materially different candidate; absence of a write does not waive that obligation.

Nominate preservation only against real change pressure and after checking the
incumbent's allocated laws. Return `unresolved` for missing evidence or incomparable
adequate candidates. Obstruction requires the evidence in
[the entry contract](SKILL.md#evidence-and-obstruction), not merely a failed attempt,
a missing universal proof, an unavailable test, or unranked tradeoffs.

A candidate must account for required valid behavior as well as forbidden states,
permitted operations and re-entry, future observations, authority, effects,
resources, compatibility, migration, and residual check owners. Removing a check
requires discharging its distinct obligation, not just giving a fact a type.
One semantic owner need not mean one process, physical service, or deployment.
Lowered caller burden does not prove representation independence, universality,
performance, or distributed correctness. Preserve claim-appropriate evidence.

Architectonic must challenge the nomination and its interactions with other
nominations; neither a route name nor a supporting result grants acceptance,
review credit, selection for implementation, or mutation authority. Do not run a
nested team, invoke Actuating, create a durable Ledger decision/plan/root receipt,
or edit code or design. This assessment makes recommendations, not independently
adopted decisions: standalone durability mechanics do not activate merely because
the recommendation is consequential or will appear in a report.

When new review evidence changes a material premise, return the affected delta
and discriminator to the nomination owner for re-adjudication. Reuse unchanged
arguments silently; do not reopen unrelated boundaries or select a successor.
