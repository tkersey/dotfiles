# System dynamics at a boundary

Use after an existing Universalist trigger when a boundary claim depends on
accumulation, feedback, adaptive policy, or recovery. Architectonic may reuse
this reasoning within its current operational investigation without invoking
Universalist unless a semantic boundary decision is live. This is ordinary
causal knowledge, not an advanced construction card or a new activation gate.
The receiving workflow retains selection, experiments, mutation, and closure.

## Separate explanation from enforcement

The boundary needed to explain a failure may be wider than the owner needed to
prevent it. Name the required observation first: bounded local storage, bounded
aggregate work, a deadline, progress, and recovery are different claims.

For an input, sink, resource, or actor assumed independent, ask whether the
candidate's behavior changes it within the relevant horizon. Include that response
only when it can alter the nomination or falsify its assumptions. Track displaced
work and effects across callers and consumers; a bounded queue can move backlog
into caller retries. Causal reach neither merges ownership nor grants authority.
Stop expanding when another dependency cannot change the affected decision.

## Reconstruct only the dynamics that matter

- **Accumulated state:** identify what persists, its units, initial condition,
  material inflows and outflows, and what regulates those flows. A reserve can
  absorb a burst without increasing sustainable throughput. Do not turn quality
  or confidence into a numerical stock without an operational definition.
- **Feedback:** trace a consequential observation through its age/interpretation,
  decision policy, action authority, and resulting state change. Check whether
  the response counteracts or amplifies the condition. A dashboard or complete
  sensor-to-action path alone proves neither effective control nor stability.
- **Timing and interaction:** include pending actions whose effects are not yet
  observed, relevant delays, saturation, and competing controllers. When evidence
  suggests changing dominance or the candidate may cross a relevant regime,
  identify the supported condition under which the response changes. Do not assume
  a relationship measured in one regime survives the proposed intervention.

Use an existing trace, transition rule, counterexample, or bound where sufficient.
A stock/flow diagram, differential equation, simulation, or second report is not
required. Keep the resulting mechanism, discriminator, and assumptions in the
existing candidate argument.

## Lower to the smallest sufficient owner

Compare native admission/reservation ownership, explicit pending state, a policy
parameter, cancellation, backpressure, or a local transition before proposing a
controller framework. Close local feedback where its consequences are local;
coordinate shared budgets or conflicting effects where their law requires it.
A single semantic authority does not require centralized physical execution.

For an evidenced adaptive requirement, separate the hard law from the policy
allowed to vary and the authority allowed to revise it. A budget or authorization
law can constrain dynamic model selection without fixing one model forever.
Use a constant or ordinary branch when it suffices; adaptation is not a license
to revise the accepted Goal, resource ceiling, or access controls.

Preserve required disturbances, degradation, and recovery behavior when removing
slack or restoring an owner. Nominally identical observations do not establish
equivalence under a permitted interruption or future continuation. Keep necessary
pending state even when it does not change the current display.

## Worked discriminator: lawful retries, growing work

Suppose each request is valid and idempotent, but timeouts cause retries that
increase load, delay completion, and cause more timeouts. Identify the actual
coupling; repeated failure alone does not prove this explanation. A queue bound
does not bound scheduled or in-flight attempts elsewhere, and idempotency of the
result does not make attempts resource-free.

Test a prediction that distinguishes this loop from fixed insufficient capacity
or malformed requests. An ordinary admission budget shared by original and retry
attempts may bound outstanding work if every relevant admission and release is
covered. That bound does not itself establish successful completion or a deadline;
retain service, scheduling, and workload assumptions for those claims. Blocking
all work does not preserve required-valid behavior.

For a malformed argument at an otherwise adequate checked constructor, resolve
the local defect without expanding into a system model. Retain the incumbent when
its existing owner already establishes the required claim and assumptions.

## Claim limits and invalidation

Separate a structural bound, a conditional response model, observed recovery,
and a stability/progress claim. State the disturbance range, horizon, and external
assumptions actually needed; sampled trajectories remain bounded evidence.
Name the workload, timing, policy, resource, or dependency change that would
invalidate the argument. An unknown rate or missing observation is evidence debt,
not an obstruction or permission to invent a controller.
