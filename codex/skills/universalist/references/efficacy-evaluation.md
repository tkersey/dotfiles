# Universalist efficacy evaluation

Use this reference only while tuning or auditing Universalist. Do not load it
during ordinary architecture work.

## Bind the evaluated subject

Every result must identify:

```text
repository and immutable repository state
Universalist source fingerprint and commit
model and effective prompt/instruction set
receiving workflow and companion skills
session or root-lineage identifier
```

Do not combine materially different Universalist versions into one efficacy
rate. Quotient worker continuations into their root lineage and separate
injected mandate text from observed decision effects.

## Measure routing before outcome

Evaluate implicit routing with
`routing-evaluation-cases.json` and repository-derived holdout cases.

Keep distinct:

```text
true activation
false activation
missed activation
correct nonactivation
preserve against live pressure
nonactivation with no disposition
```

Routing precision over activation-bearing lineages does not measure recall.
Sample nonactivated boundary decisions independently to estimate missed latent
laws, owner errors, representation errors, and fired invalidators.

## Paired contribution ablation

Replay the same repository state and accepted evidence under three blinded
conditions:

```text
A. No Universalist
B. Routine kernel only
   trigger evidence + incoming candidate + owner + observations + law +
   falsifier + ordinary candidate
C. Full Universalist
   routine kernel + gated latent recognition + eligible construction cards
```

Keep all non-Universalist instructions and evidence fixed. Do not let condition
labels appear in the candidate output presented to evaluators.

Evaluate the resulting architecture on:

- semantic-owner correctness;
- preservation of required behavior and observations;
- exclusion of invalid states and sanctioned bypasses;
- legal composition and transition semantics;
- compatibility and migration safety;
- failure honesty and residual ownership;
- falsifier and proof strength;
- repository-native simplicity and resource cost.

Interpret paired results as follows:

```text
B > A
  evidence that the routine Universalist kernel adds decision value

C > B
  evidence that latent recognition or advanced mechanics add incremental value

C == B with an advanced card rejected by ordinary dominance
  a valid protective result, but not evidence for expanding the card corpus
```

A named theorem, category-shaped vocabulary, or successful merge is not an
incremental-effect witness. A retained advanced mechanic must change the
repository-native artifact, legal composition, migration, or proof compared
with the ordinary candidate.

## Corpus evolution

Freeze additions to the construction-card corpus until repeated paired cases
show a concrete `C > B` artifact or proof dividend. This does not remove or
disable the existing cards. It prevents theoretical inventory growth from
substituting for demonstrated architectural utility.

For every full-path activation record:

```text
trigger evidence
incoming incumbent or ordinary candidate
material decision delta
mechanic: ordinary | card:<id>
card dispositions and ordinary-dominance rejections
resulting repository-native artifact or obstruction
falsifier and invalidators
```

## Report four independent results

1. **Routing precision** — useful implicit activations among all implicit
   activations.
2. **Routing recall** — detected live boundary decisions among an independently
   sampled set of live boundary decisions.
3. **Incremental decision efficacy** — paired `B > A` results.
4. **Advanced-mechanics efficacy** — paired `C > B` results with a concrete
   artifact or proof delta.

Report sample size and uncertainty. Decision-effect statements nested inside a
root lineage are not independent trials.
