# Universalist

**Generalize in the reasoning. Specialize in the realization. Prove the connection.**

Use relevant algebraic and categorical knowledge to formulate the problem and derive
its solution, not merely recognize a structure after an architecture has been chosen.
Seek constructions and transformations that make required behavior structural,
remove accidental choices or repeated obligations, or eliminate unnecessary work.
Lower to repository-native code, not a categorical framework by default.

<a id="boundary-decision-trigger-uni-boundary"></a>

## Semantic-derivation trigger (`UNI-BOUNDARY`)

Explicit `$universalist` invocation always activates this skill. Implicit use applies
when the task needs semantic reasoning about behavior, representation, algorithms,
composition, observations, effects, ownership, or proof. Required uses, a question
about an implementation, or an unexplained burden are sufficient grounds for inquiry.
A distributed law, a live boundary choice, and a fired invalidator remain triggers;
they are not prerequisites for discovering the formulation. `UNI-BOUNDARY` retains
its identifier for existing consumers; its scope includes semantic derivation.

Do not wait for duplication, an ordinary implementation to fail, a preidentified
owner or typed hole, an already-derived challenger, or permission to consider a
particular theory. An accepted public interface need not settle its algorithm,
internal representation, or proof. Purely mechanical realization of a settled
construction, unchanged check execution, formatting, review bookkeeping, rebasing,
`$ship`, and `$land` do not require renewed inquiry. The distinction is unresolved
semantic work, not file type, diff size, or whether an edit is owner-local.

Reuse adequate derivations and unchanged decisions silently. Do not invent semantic
work to activate the skill or issue a no-op receipt. Within a receiving workflow,
use its existing inquiry rather than a duplicate root pass. Team/subagent mode
remains explicit-request only.

## Generative common path

Inside Actuating, load [actuating-composition.md](actuating-composition.md); Actuating
owns selection, experiments, mutation, and closure. That adapter owns Actuating's
presentation and inquiry composition; it does not waive claim-specific evidence.
Inside Architectonic, instead
load [architectonic-composition.md](architectonic-composition.md) for a read-only
nomination in its existing assessment. Neither composition adopts a standalone
plan or root receipt.

Ground the inquiry in required uses and observations, permitted future behavior,
actual code or attributed proposed design, effects, authority, resources, and
compatibility. Distinguish accepted requirements from candidate laws, observed
regularities, and known non-laws. Existing implementation is evidence, not automatic
requirement authority. At first use, derive from requirements without inventing an
incumbent or historical examples.

**What formulation makes the required behavior a consequence of the construction,
and can a different formulation eliminate a responsibility altogether?** Treat
model-owned carriers, operation vocabularies, decompositions, interpretations, axes,
typed holes, and proof strategies as revisable hypotheses. Accepted outcomes,
source-fixed architecture, authorized effects, and resource ceilings stay binding.

Reason in both directions: concrete operations and observations suggest structures;
mathematical knowledge suggests hypotheses, consequences, and discriminating
questions. A theory may generate the hypothesis of its own applicability; it cannot
certify that hypothesis. A task-grounded question is enough to consult a focused
reference. Develop laws, alternatives, and discriminators during inquiry rather
than demand the finished discovery as its admission condition.

Use whichever constructive move advances the problem: change a carrier to preserve
necessary distinctions and remove accidental ones; derive an orthogonal operation
basis; change the unit of composition; reify, interpret, specialize, or fuse behavior;
derive a sufficient observation with residual state; or replace repeated checking
with a preservation argument. Let contradictions revise candidate laws or the
formulation, never silently weaken accepted requirements. These are available moves,
not a checklist, a separate pass, or a candidate quota.

Use the incumbent and ordinary native candidates as comparison baselines, not a
prescribed chronology of thought. A baseline may fail the requirements; record its
unmet obligations rather than inventing an adequate alternative. A record, tagged union, checked constructor,
adapter, parameter, state machine, operation IR, handler, graph, query, loop, merge,
or witness may be the best result of sophisticated reasoning. Intermediate
mathematical structure may disappear in specialization. An unlisted construction
or combination of ideas is investigable under the same evidence standards; the
atlas and registry are retrieval aids, not an exhaustive list of permitted ideas.

Reuse supplied candidates and sufficient arguments. Follow task-relevant questions
while their resolution can improve the construction or deciding proof; stop when the
required result is adequately supported and no material question remains open.
Do not claim that all possible alternatives have been excluded. Neither speculative
corpus enumeration nor a fixed search quota establishes sufficiency. Preserve an
adequate incumbent without manufacturing a challenger or change.

## Earn the nomination

Inquiry is not acceptance. Before retaining a candidate, establish its actual
correspondence to the task, applicable laws and non-laws, distinguishing
counterexample or proof, required-valid preservation, effects, feasibility, and
engineering dividend. The dividend may be a simpler representation or algorithm,
removed coordination, a lawful transformation, a reusable proof, or a justified
preservation result; it need not create a new boundary or public abstraction.
Compare materially different candidates under [consequential-boundary.md](references/consequential-boundary.md)
before recommending or performing an affected change. A hypothesis may be provisional;
a selected construction may not borrow its missing premises from its name.

Keep interacting laws together: admission with permitted mutation, observations
with future transitions, and descriptions with effects and resources. Identify
actual enforcement and residual owners before claiming adequacy. A checked
constructor does not prove preservation under aliases, decoding, re-entry, or
lifetime changes. Preserve required-valid behavior as well as excluding invalid
behavior. One coherent relationship is the unit of reasoning, not a limit on
completion of an already authorized multi-seam task.

For representation choices, determine whether consumers need inspectable data or
only its elimination. Remove materialization only when inspection, sharing,
persistence, authorization, identity, replay, and rewriting are absent or preserved.
Native data, folds, and loops remain valid outcomes. Church-style callbacks alone
prove neither parametricity, initiality, confidentiality, nor a performance gain.

For interfaces, compare complete caller contracts through a representative use and
source-supported change: knowledge, choices, sequencing, synchronization, and cleanup
removed; change localized; and costs introduced. Fewer exports, relocated duties,
and hidden configuration do not establish information hiding. Lower caller burden
is not a universality or representation-independence proof.

When accumulation, feedback, adaptive policy, or recovery matters, consult
[system-dynamics.md](references/system-dynamics.md). Trace the necessary causal
extent without automatically merging enforcement owners or escalating mechanics.
A local admission bound does not prove aggregate progress or stability.

“Smallest” is relative to required behavior and observations, compatibility,
authority, effects, resources, host capabilities, caller obligations, and migration
cost—not source length, export count, or mathematical sophistication. Do not claim
dominance across unranked material tradeoffs.

## Route discipline

Assign a route only after lowering or establishing a claim-scoped obstruction:

- `UNI-PRESERVE` — an adequate incumbent resolves the live semantic question or
  survives change pressure; do not emit ambient preservation ceremony.
- `UNI-ORDINARY` — an adequate native construction or transformation closes the
  question, even when sophisticated mathematics derived it.
- `UNI-CANONICAL` — a retained advanced construction materially improves the native
  baseline with a complete claim-appropriate effective witness. The name does not
  assert universality or require a registry entry.
- `UNI-OBSTRUCT` — evidence closes constructive alternatives for the affected
  required claim; no honest representable or effective artifact satisfies it.

Record the actual mechanism as `ordinary`, `card:<id>` when a registered card is
used, or `derived:<name>` for an unregistered retained construction. The last is
attribution, not a new route, proof exemption, or requirement to grow the registry.
A useful mathematical derivation that disappears in native lowering can remain
`ordinary`; intellectual sophistication does not determine the route.

Missing evidence and incomparable adequate candidates remain **unresolved** or
**underdetermined**, not `UNI-OBSTRUCT`. These are supporting outcomes, not new
routes. Obstruction to a demanded canonical choice does not obstruct an adequate
weaker construction or discharge the stronger requirement. Primitive bypasses
require contained authority, failure behavior, and residuals; their existence alone
is not an impossibility proof. Do not add `UNI-SKIP`.

`references/decision-contract.json` owns machine-readable trigger, route, and
clause identifiers. Change semantic policy, contract, and durable plan template
together. Existing identifiers are retained; new runs bind the current fingerprint,
while historical receipts remain attributed to their original contracts.

## Composition and reclassification

Inside Actuating, contribute directly under its adapter; `candidate`,
`preserve-incumbent`, `unresolved`, and `obstructed` remain optional presentation.
Inside Architectonic, return those results under its unchanged read-only adapter.
Supply requirements, observations, evidence, and constraints; owner, axis, and
typed hole can be derived rather than required on entry. The receiving workflow
selects or adjudicates. Discovery grants no mutation, experiment, nested team,
review credit, closure, or standalone durability authority.

When new evidence may change requirements, owner, observations, compatibility,
effects, resources, formulation, law, proof, enforcement, route, or decomposition,
read [reclassification.md](reclassification.md) before the next affected mutation.
Reuse unchanged decisions silently; never independently reopen a receiving
workflow's selected target or overwrite a root receipt.

## Evidence and obstruction

Distinguish evidenced true, evidenced absent, unknown/not inspected,
underdetermined, and witnessed obstruction. Obstruction requires attributed
counterevidence, a reproducible counterexample, stability under comparison maps,
an effectivity account, a falsifier, a reopening condition, and resolution of
active constructive alternatives. A failed attempt is not nonexistence.

In standalone work, obtain missing evidence through the smallest relevant
inspection or already-authorized safe discriminator. Uninspected code is not
unavailable evidence. Genuinely missing access, evidence, or authority yields debt
and a next discriminator, not invented policy, capability, coherence, or a winner.
Do not run unauthorized or external-effect experiments. Inside Actuating, return
the discriminator; Actuating owns experimentation and review-epoch rules. Block
only the affected decision and continue independent authorized work.

## Output contract

Complete the requested kind and scope of work. Analysis returns a supported
nomination or unresolved result; authorized implementation includes required
verification, callers, and migration. A first witness does not prove coverage of
every sanctioned path or complete a multi-seam request. Do not widen work merely
because a general construction expresses more. In a receiving workflow, the
applicable adapter owns the contribution's form; that workflow retains execution
and completion authority. A contribution does not complete the enclosing task.

Use repository and domain language. Convey the material question, resulting
construction and owner, baseline when present, required observations, law and
falsifier, decision consequence, mechanism, and invalidators once through existing
evidence. These are conclusion obligations, not prerequisites to inquiry, mandatory
headings, or a second report. Do not invent an incumbent, delta, or final route.
Surface useful consequences, not a transcript of the reasoning procedure or a
list of theories considered. Categorical names and proof detail belong only where
requested or necessary to justify the claim. Exact serialization is required only
by an actual consumer, such as the standalone durable contract.
