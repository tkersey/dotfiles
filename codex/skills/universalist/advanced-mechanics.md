# Advanced mechanics

<a id="advanced-mechanics-gate"></a>

## Inquiry and acceptance are different thresholds

Use relevant mathematical knowledge to formulate a task-grounded question as
well as to solve one. Consult the artifact selector or a focused registry fragment
when it can help discover the carrier, operation vocabulary, composition, typed
hole, or deciding proof. A failed ordinary candidate, completed consequential
comparison, and preidentified construction are not prerequisites for inquiry.

The registry and atlas are retrieval aids, not an exhaustive list of permitted
ideas. An unlisted construction or a combination of known ideas can be investigated
without adding a card. Read precise theory references when their hypotheses or
consequences matter; do not browse the entire corpus to manufacture relevance.
Reuse sufficient derivations instead of running another pass.

## Retaining a construction

Before recommending or adopting a candidate:

1. Establish the required behavior, observations, actual correspondence, necessary
   interacting laws, effects, and resources. Revise a model-owned axis or typed
   hole when the derivation reveals a better formulation.
2. Compare its complete native realization with the incumbent when present and
   ordinary native candidates. A baseline may be inadequate; preserve its unmet
   obligations in the comparison rather than require an adequate competitor. Use
   consequential comparison when materially different candidates are live; the
   inquiry itself need not wait for it.
3. Discharge actual prerequisites and the claim-appropriate witness, using the
   relevant card's exact `theory_refs` when a card is used. For an unlisted retained
   construction, supply the same hypotheses, interpretation, discriminator,
   feasibility, and proof; record `derived:<name>`, not an invented card identifier.
4. Classify consulted candidates as selected, rejected, contradicted, or unresolved
   in the existing argument. Lower a retained candidate into native code or a
   transformation; mathematical structure may disappear during specialization.

Stop when the task's construction and deciding proof are adequately supported
and no material question remains. There is no fixed card count or obligation to
prove that all possible alternatives have been enumerated.

Match proof to the actual claim: a lawful structure needs its laws and
interpretation, not an invented universal property. Mediation and uniqueness
apply only to a named universal construction with specified competitors and maps.
Opacity, one public interpreter, and passing examples alone establish neither.
A bounded approximation states its domain, losses, and evidence limits. A route
name, `derived` attribution, or registry membership never upgrades claim strength.

Signals, registry order, `diagnostic_order`, evidence count, and theorem
sophistication never prove prerequisites or select a route. Prefer the least
burdensome adequate realization, not the least sophisticated derivation. Native
lowering can remain `UNI-ORDINARY`; preserving an adequate incumbent is success.
