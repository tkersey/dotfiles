# Prepared architectural review

Load only when parallel investigations are useful. One coherent obligation can be
reviewed directly under the entry and investigation contracts; do not create
workers merely to satisfy a count. Once parallel preparation is selected, do not
silently replace failed forks with a claimed equivalent direct or summary-only
review. Retain useful evidence and report the affected coverage as incomplete.

## Shared preparation and immutable seed

Use the installed Elenctic [native-fork transport](../../elenctic/references/native-forks.md)
for capability discovery, actual source-thread resolution, persistent transport,
full observable-history verification, and source/seed/worker parent provenance.
This is transport reuse, not an invocation of Elenctic or its campaign. Here its
Campaign Brief means Architectonic's orientation, its epoch means the subject
binding below, and its canonical assignment means the assignment below. No file
selection, Viewed state, thread adjudication, Elenctic worker contract, or Actuating
review credit is imported. CAS, when needed, supplies transport only.

Resolve the available native route before deep preparation; an absent wrapper is
not proof that native forks are unavailable. Preserve the actual model, reasoning
effort, working directory, and permissions. Do not change configuration to obtain
a capability. Missing source/history/provenance is an explicit coverage failure,
not permission to invent a seed or use a fresh generic subagent.

The coordinator reconstructs the whole selected causal scope once and publishes
the entry contract's source-bound orientation in the transcript. Include a compact
inventory of selected obligations, their material interactions, and assignment
ownership. Existing component boundaries are hypotheses, not assignment borders.
Facts and accepted requirements cite immutable evidence; hypotheses include a
falsifier and no preselected repair or adjudicated consequence.

Bind the preparation to the exact subject, source/document identities, included
working-tree content, accepted requirement versions, selected obligations and
interactions, and installed contract versions. Record a real orientation digest
or runtime content identity; that identity covers the orientation bytes, not opaque
reasoning internals or independent judgment.

Create one immutable seed from the coordinator only after preparation. Verify
that it retains the prepared history through the orientation, including the
preparation turn, and its exact parent ID. Fork every investigator directly from
that unchanged seed and verify the same retained history and parent edge before
assignment. A fork that ends before preparation, a copied orientation, or a fork
of the evolving coordinator is not equivalent. Readback verifies observable
history; never claim to inspect opaque reasoning or expose raw inherited content.

Give the seed no assignments, findings, follow-ups, or results. Shared history is
orientation, not authority: each worker revalidates its premises, including prior
implementation rationales. Correlated agreement does not add independent proof.

## Assign by obligation

Use the following information in each assignment; omit no material binding:

```text
Role: read-only Architectonic investigator, not a coordinator.
Subject and preparation identity: <exact bound snapshot and orientation identity>.
Seed and assignment identity: <actual seed ID; coordinator-assigned identifier>.
Obligation / interacting law family: <accepted outcome and source>.
Causal scope and known interactions: <paths and relevant scenarios, not a file fence>.
Nomination ownership: <this decision, or the other named assignment that owns it>.
Installed contracts: <resolved Architectonic entry, investigation, and Universalist binding>.
Task: verify premises locally; trace the obligation end to end; challenge incumbent
and any admissible alternative; try to falsify retained conclusions; return the
assignment/binding identities, findings, evidence, actual check outcomes, residuals,
interaction consequences, and exact covered/uninspected obligations.
Authority: no writes/publication, coordinator entry points, spawning, or nested teams.
```

An investigator may use Universalist inline only for its owned live decision under
Architectonic composition; it must not delegate again. Shared decisions get one
nomination owner, not competing duplicate Universalist passes. Other workers
report interaction evidence to the coordinator. Do not artificially separate laws
whose validity depends on each other.

Keep at most 20 investigators active, or the caller's lower positive limit. Match
actual supported capacity; admit work on completion and replenish rather than
launching everything or imposing a limit on causal exploration. Track actual
thread/turn IDs, assignment, subject/preparation identity, and terminal state.
A timeout or transport failure is not semantic completion. On uncertain delivery
or reconnect, recover the exact turn state before retrying; do not duplicate work.

## Admission and synthesis

Read the exact terminal report. Match its worker, turn, seed lineage, preparation,
subject, assignment, and contract identities. Inspect the supporting evidence and
check semantics, not merely a successful envelope or the worker's chosen label.
Reject mismatched, stale, missing, truncated, or unbound completion as coverage.
Keep independently verifiable facts as evidence, without upgrading an unadmitted
report to a completed assignment.

A valid complete report may contain defects, risk, or missing mandatory proof;
transport/provenance validity does not grant acceptance. A worker's scoped
completion also does not establish another worker's obligation or a cross-worker
interaction. The coordinator owns adjudication and must inspect material
interactions itself or assign a bounded follow-up tied to the same evidence.
A follow-up that receives findings is not another independent seed-based review;
label its lineage and re-adjudication purpose honestly.

Use the entry contract's reconciliation and output rules. Withhold a complete
assessment while any selected material obligation or interaction lacks valid
coverage; preserve verified findings alongside the missing coverage. No file count,
Viewed marker, unanimous verdict, or passed optional check substitutes for it.

Changes to material subject inputs, requirement versions, selected scope, installed
contracts, or orientation invalidate affected preparation and coverage. Do not
silently patch the seed or mix epochs. Reprepare and create a fresh seed for new
parallel work; previous findings are hypotheses until revalidated. A lost source
or seed must be recovered through exact runtime provenance, not a title or latest
session guess. No persistent campaign ledger or new executable is required.
