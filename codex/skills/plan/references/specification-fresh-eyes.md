# Specification Fresh-Eyes Pass

Before architecture-policy synthesis, reread the governed specification from the
original objective, repository evidence, Evidence Brief, decision-completeness
state, and Architectonic Thread. Do not trust the path that produced it.

Look for:

- objective drift or missing non-goals;
- smuggled implementation or source-fixed decisions;
- consequential architecture hidden in implementation prose;
- file-shaped factorization or architecture theater;
- abstraction proliferation, duplicate owners, or shadow truth;
- validators, caches, correlation, compatibility branches, or bypasses that
  reconstruct information the representation should retain;
- sequence items that realize quotiented, ablated, normalized, or superseded factors;
- migration or rollback that loses required observations;
- missing law, falsifier, residual obligation, or invalidator;
- a failed specification square where process and architecture both compose;
- vague, scaffold-only, stale, or non-artifact-bound proof;
- requirements without owner, enforcement, and proof traceability;
- plan-shaped action waves leaking into the specification;
- defaults presented as accepted user decisions;
- unresolved material judgment or unaccounted delegated work.

Rules:

- Revise only affected sections and all downstream derivations.
- An architectonic change regenerates every section derived from the changed seam.
- A missing material decision returns to judgment acquisition or blocks; it is not
  silently invented during planning.
- Continue to policy synthesis only after one zero-material-delta reread.
- Keep the pass internal; do not emit a receipt or readiness certificate.
