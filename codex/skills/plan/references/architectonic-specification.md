# Architectonic Specification

Recover the smallest coherent organization whose factors own live obligations,
whose truths have canonical owners, and whose construction excludes the supported
invalid family while preserving required-valid behavior.

## Admission

Use this reasoning when plausible organizations materially differ in behavior,
ownership, compatibility, migration, enforcement, information retention, resources,
or proof. Touching a type, file, module, or API is not sufficient. Local work inside
an already-exact boundary may retain it with one law and falsifier.

## Seam and authority

For each consequential seam identify one independently governed axis and typed hole,
the semantic owner and boundary, live obligations, required observations, compatibility,
effects and resource constraints, incumbent, selected or conditioned organization,
factor dispositions, law, falsifier, residuals, and invalidators.

```text
source_fixed         user-required organization or forbidden alternative
source_bounded       revisable means inside required behavior and constraints
specification_local  necessary delegated choice without changing accepted semantics
```

`specification_local` lowers to `plan_local` only in EPG export. A mechanism selected
by Plan remains revisable within its envelope unless current authority explicitly
fixes it. Accepting a detailed plan does not silently lock all its implementation
suggestions.

Use linked seams for independent pressures rather than one global architectural
winner. An evidence-conditioned seam names exact deciding observations, admissible
alternatives, forbidden results, and a safe default or blocker. Underdetermination
without a lawful observation route or authority owner blocks affected work.

## Candidate comparison

Recover obligations before accepting existing files, classes, layers, or services as
real factors. State the ordinary repository-native candidate first, then compare:

```text
preservation
admitted-domain restriction
representation or owner strengthening
ablation or normalization
```

A candidate dominates only if no weaker on required behavior, observations,
compatibility, enforcement, proof, effects, and resources, while reducing accidental
distinctions, duplicate owners, invalid states, bypasses, reconstruction paths, or
unearned factors. Fewer lines or files alone proves nothing. Removing required-valid
behavior is a requirement change, not a better construction.

Classify each factor's obligation as live, moved, expired, duplicated, invalid, or
unknown; disposition the factor as preserve, factor, quotient, ablate, normalize, or
introduce. Retained factors must earn live obligations. Displaced factors must be
retired with their replacements or retain a distinct, explicit compatibility duty.

## Proof and derivation

Use action-contract.md for family exclusion, valid-domain preservation, independent
coverage, discriminator, and claim strength. Identify admission, ownership,
transitions, lifetime, and bypass closure, not just the constructor's name. Record
how execution will discover the real verification domain independently of the
candidate's asserted list. A residual is owned and bounded, not quietly deleted.

Selected seams determine the implementation approach, actions, enforcement loci,
migration/retirement, proof, rollback, and done-state. Changing a seam invalidates
its derived work. Use architectonic-policy-synthesis.md for transport and
policy-synthesis-fixed-point.md for refinement; do not add an architectonic loop.

Reject architecture theater, file-shaped factorization, shadow truth, reconstruction
compensators, source-authority drift, elegance without migration, and a sequence
that still realizes an ablated or superseded organization.
